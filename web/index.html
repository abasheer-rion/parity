The wall between them

Three rules, written into how Parity is built.
  • 1Research decides, the app presents.Every number in the app comes from research, with its verdict next to it.
  • 2Popularity never counts.Sign-ups, watchlists and clicks never reach the research side or any test.
  • 3Every try is counted.The more ideas tested, the higher the bar each must clear. Searching harder cannot make a winner.
