Five verdicts, said plainly.

  • Holds upMakes money after costs and clears the luck check for the number of tries behind it.
  • Could be luckMakes money, but the result does not clear the luck check for the tries behind it.
  • FragileMakes money on average, but one bad run of losses could erase the gains.
  • Does not hold upLoses money on average after costs, or the file contradicts the claim.
  • Not enough dataFewer than 30 trades or 60 daily returns. Parity says nothing until there is more.
