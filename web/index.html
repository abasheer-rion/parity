The wall between them

Three rules keep the results honest.

  • Research decides, the app presents.Every number in the app comes from research, with its verdict next to it.
  • Popularity never counts.Sign-ups, watchlists and clicks never reach the research side or any test.
  • Every try is counted.The more ideas tested, the higher the bar each must clear. Searching harder cannot make a winner.

How an idea becomes a result

The same path for every strategy, whoever thought of it.

  1. An ideaFrom a paper, a trader's practice, or the always-on search.
  2. Written down firstWhat we expect, and what would prove it wrong, before any test.
  3. The gauntletCosts, other periods, other settings, and a check for luck.
  4. Shown with its verdictIn plain words, claiming no more than the research found.
