# Parity wordmark, 2026-10-22

Chosen by the team on 2026-10-22: the version on the light blue background, bottom
left of the sheet. It pairs the starburst, a thin divider, PARITY in Roman capitals
and SYSTEMATIC RESEARCH beneath.

- `parity-lockup-on-blue.svg` is the chosen version, on its light blue gradient.
- `parity-lockup-on-light.svg` and `parity-lockup-on-dark.svg` are the same lockup without a background.
- `parity-wordmark-*.svg` drop the subtitle, for smaller spaces.
- `parity-letters-*.svg` hold PARITY alone, for the app sidebar next to the separate mark.

Letters are drawn as paths from Cinzel (SIL Open Font License), so no font is needed.
Rebuild with `scripts/brand_wordmark.py`. The starburst comes from `scripts/brand.py`.
