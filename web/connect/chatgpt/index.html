The address

1. Add Parity to ChatGPT

Adding your own server is done on chatgpt.com in a browser. Once added, it also works in the ChatGPT apps. OpenAI calls these plugins now; older menus say apps or connectors. On a work or school workspace, your admin decides whether custom servers are allowed.

  1. Go to Plugins on chatgpt.com (chatgpt.com/plugins). If your menu still says Settings, then Apps and connectors, turn on Developer mode under Advanced first.
  2. Choose the plus button, then Add custom MCP server (or Create).
  3. Name it Parity, paste the address above as the server URL and choose OAuth for authentication. Leave the client ID and secret empty.
  4. Read OpenAI's warning about custom servers and choose I understand and want to continue, then Create.
  5. ChatGPT opens Parity's log-in page. Log in, check the Allow page names ChatGPT and chatgpt.com, and choose Allow.
  6. Back in ChatGPT, Parity shows in your plugins with its tools listed.

2. Use it in a chat

  1. In the message box, type @ and choose Parity, or pick it from the plus menu.
  2. Ask, for example, "Use Parity to find tested carry strategies and tell me which ones passed the most tests."
  3. ChatGPT shows which Parity tool it wants to use. It asks you first before any tool that changes something.

6. Disconnect

  • From Parity: open Connect your AI, find ChatGPT under "Your connected apps" and choose Disconnect. ChatGPT's next call fails and it has to ask you to log in again.
  • From ChatGPT: open your plugins (or Settings, then Apps and connectors), choose Parity and remove or disconnect it.
  • To stop changes but stay connected, untick "May make changes" for ChatGPT on Connect your AI.

7. If it does not work

  • No way to add a custom server. Your plan or workspace may not allow it, or developer mode is off. On a work workspace, ask your admin.
  • The log-in page does not open. Check the address ends in /mcp and that OAuth is chosen, not "No authentication".
  • "This request has expired or is not valid". The sign-in link is single use. Start again from ChatGPT.
  • ChatGPT says it cannot place an order. Changes are off. Tick "May make changes" for ChatGPT on Connect your AI. Orders are demo orders only.
  • "Too many calls". You reached 30 calls in a minute or 1000 in a day. Wait and ask again.
  • Still stuck? Email contact@paritytrading.app with the time it failed and the message ChatGPT showed.
