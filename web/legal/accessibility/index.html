Accessibility statement

Draft for lawyer review Last updated 22 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use Parity, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or other assistive technology.

Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We have not had an independent audit, and we do not claim full conformance yet. The known gaps are listed below.

What we have done so far

  • The public pages, sign-in pages and apps can be used with a keyboard. They show a visible focus outline and have a "Skip to content" link.
  • Form fields have labels. Errors are written out in words and read aloud by screen readers.
  • Passwords can be shown while you type, and password rules are listed as you go.
  • Text and controls are checked automatically for colour contrast.
  • Pages use headings and landmarks, so you can jump between sections.
  • Motion stops when your device asks for reduced motion. The 3D picture on the front page becomes a still image.
  • We check pages on narrow phone screens for sideways scrolling. Wide data tables scroll inside their own box instead.

How we test

We run automated checks with axe-core on the public pages, the sign-in pages and both app home pages, and fix every serious and critical problem it finds. We also check pages by hand with a keyboard. Last checked on 22 October 2026.

Known gaps

  • Charts. Many charts are pictures of data. The key numbers are usually written nearby, but not every chart has a text or table version yet.
  • Sim World's map. The world map is visual and hard to use with a screen reader. A text alternative is planned.
  • Dense tables. Some research tables are wide and need sideways scrolling on small screens.
  • Research papers and outside links. Papers and sites we link to are not ours, and some are not accessible.
  • Colour in charts. Some charts use colour to tell lines apart. Adding labels and patterns is planned.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on Parity is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us. Say which page, what you were trying to do, and what technology you use, if you are happy to share it.

Email contact@paritytrading.ai. We aim to reply within 5 working days, and we can send you the information in another format.

If you are not happy with our reply

In the United States, you can contact the Department of Justice about the Americans with Disabilities Act. In the UK, the Equality Advisory and Support Service can help. In the EU, contact the body that enforces accessibility law in your country.
