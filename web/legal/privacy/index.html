1. Who we are

Parity is a research and education service about trading strategies. It runs at parity.endyland.com and sim.endyland.com. In this policy, "we" means the operator of Parity: owner: add the legal name and postal address of the person or company that runs Parity.

For data protection law, we are the controller of the personal data described here.

2. What we collect

When you make an account

  • Your email address. You sign in with it, and we send sign-in emails to it.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled hash. Supabase, our sign-in provider, stores it. Nobody at Parity can read it.
  • Sign-in records. When you sign in or change your account, Supabase records the time, your IP address and your browser type. This helps stop break-ins.
  • Two-step sign-in, if you turn it on. Supabase stores the secret your authenticator app uses, and any passkeys you add.
  • Google or GitHub sign-in, if you use it. We receive your email address and basic profile details (such as your name) from that service.
  • A deletion request, if you make one. Your account ID, email and any note you write.

When you use the site

  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in. See the cookie policy.
  • Settings kept in your browser, such as your theme, bookmarks, saved scenarios and Practice mode's pretend portfolio. They stay in your browser. We do not receive them.
  • Server logs. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, processes each request: your IP address, the address you asked for, the time and your browser type. We use these only to run and protect the site.
  • Font requests. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser type when those fonts load. owner: consider hosting the fonts ourselves to remove this

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no tracking pixels.
  • No payment details. Parity does not take money.
  • No brokerage or bank account details. Parity does not trade.
  • No identity documents.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account exists. After you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days. Copies in our providers' backups expire on their schedule. owner: confirm the Supabase backup period for this plan
  • Sign-in records: as long as Supabase keeps auth logs for our plan, then they are deleted.
  • Server logs: as long as Cloudflare keeps them under its own policy. We do not export or store them ourselves.
  • Emails: Resend keeps delivery records for a limited time under its own policy.
  • The sign-in cookie: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Browser storage: until you clear it in your browser.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal data;
  • correct it;
  • have it deleted;
  • limit or object to how we use it;
  • get it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent;
  • complain to a data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office. In the EU it is the authority where you live or work.

You can change your email and password, and ask for deletion, in Settings, Account. For anything else, contact us (below). We answer within one month. We may need to confirm it is really you first.

8. California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, you have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use and disclose (this policy lists it);
  • delete it;
  • correct it;
  • opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising;
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information. The only sensitive item we hold is your account login, and we use it only to sign you in;
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

In the last 12 months we collected these categories: identifiers (email, IP address), internet activity (server logs), and account login details. We collected them from you and your browser, for the purposes in section 3. You, or an agent you authorise, can make a request by contacting us. We answer within 45 days.

9. Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere. Passwords are hashed by Supabase. You can turn on two-step sign-in with an authenticator app. The database only lets each person see what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use anywhere else. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Children

Parity is not for children. You must be 18 or older to make an account. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes
