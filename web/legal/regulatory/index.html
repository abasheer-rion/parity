1. Who we are

Parity is run by Parity Trading, Inc., a Delaware corporation. You can contact us by email at contact@paritytrading.ai or by post at 7213 Continental Drive, Newark, Delaware, United States.

Parity is not registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), the National Futures Association (NFA), FINRA, any state securities regulator, or any financial regulator outside the United States. It holds no financial licence of any kind.

2. What Parity is and is not

Parity is:

  • a research site that tests trading strategies on past data and shows the results, including the tests they failed;
  • a set of courses (Parity Academy) and a news archive;
  • a checker for trading records (Parity Verify) that runs in your browser;
  • demo accounts that use pretend money and delayed prices.

Parity is not:

  • a broker-dealer. It does not place real orders, open brokerage accounts, or take pay tied to trades;
  • an investment adviser. It does not give advice about your own finances, and what it publishes is the same for everyone;
  • a commodity trading advisor. Its signals about futures, crypto perpetual futures and currencies are not tailored to anyone and do not trade anyone's account;
  • a custodian, bank, exchange or money transmitter. It never holds or moves your money or crypto;
  • a place to trade real money. Demo trading is pretend. No real trade happens.

Nothing on Parity is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. See the terms and the risk notice.

3. Why we do not hold a financial licence

Financial licences are needed for certain activities. Parity is set up so it does not carry them out. These are the laws and exemptions we rely on.

The laws and exemptions Parity relies on
RoleWhy Parity does not need to registerLaw
Investment adviser The law excludes publishers of bona fide financial publications of general and regular circulation. The Supreme Court held this covers publications that are impersonal, disinterested and regularly published (Lowe v. SEC, 472 U.S. 181 (1985)). Parity's research is the same for every reader and never advises one person. Investment Advisers Act of 1940, section 202(a)(11)(D), 15 U.S.C. 80b-2(a)(11); Lowe v. SEC
Commodity trading advisor The law excludes publishers of electronic data of general and regular dissemination. CFTC rules also exempt anyone who does not direct client accounts and does not give advice tailored to particular clients. Commodity Exchange Act, 7 U.S.C. 1a(12); CFTC rule 17 CFR 4.14(a)(9)
Broker-dealer A broker effects securities transactions for others. Parity places no real orders, takes no pay tied to trades, opens no accounts and holds no customer money or securities. Securities Exchange Act of 1934, sections 3(a)(4) and 15(a), 15 U.S.C. 78c, 15 U.S.C. 78o
Introducing broker or forex dealer Parity does not solicit or accept futures or forex orders and is not anyone's trading counterparty. Commodity Exchange Act; CFTC rules 17 CFR 5.3
Money transmitter Parity never receives or sends money or crypto for anyone. Bank Secrecy Act rules, 31 CFR 1010.100; state money transmitter laws

Outside the United States, Parity does not give personal recommendations, so it does not provide "investment advice" as the EU's MiFID II defines it (Directive 2014/65/EU, article 4(1)(4)), or advice on crypto-assets under the EU's MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114).

4. Other laws we follow

  • Honest claims. Section 5 of the FTC Act bans deceptive practices (15 U.S.C. 45). Results on Parity are labelled as backtests. A Verify badge describes a record; it is not an endorsement and does not say the record will make money. The FTC's rules on endorsements and reviews (16 CFR Part 255, 16 CFR Part 465) apply to anyone who uses a Parity badge in an advert.
  • Email. We send only sign-in and account emails. Any marketing email will follow the CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. chapter 103), with an unsubscribe link.
  • Children. Parity is for people 18 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act covers (16 CFR Part 312). If a child has given us data, we delete it.
  • Privacy. We follow the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018), the California Consumer Privacy Act (California Civil Code section 1798.100 and following), and Delaware's Online Privacy and Protection Act (6 Del. C. chapter 12C). Details are in the privacy policy.
  • Cookies. Without your consent, we store only what Parity needs to work, as the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC, article 5(3)) and the UK's PECR (regulation 6) allow. Our own analytics runs only if you switch it on. See the cookie policy.
  • Accessibility. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA, in line with the Americans with Disabilities Act (42 U.S.C. 12182). See the accessibility statement.
  • Market data. Prices on Parity come from outside sources and are used under their licences. Some are delayed or end of day.

5. Your rights

Your data

  • Everyone can ask what we hold about them, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Delete your account in Settings, Account, or email us.
  • In the EU, the EEA and the UK, you also have the rights in articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR and UK GDPR: access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawing consent. We answer within one month (article 12(3)). You can complain to a data protection authority (article 77).
  • In California, you have the rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, and limit the use of sensitive data, and not to be treated differently for using them. We do not sell or share personal information. We answer within 45 days. You can use an authorised agent.

To use any of these rights, email contact@paritytrading.ai with "Privacy request" in the subject. We may ask you to confirm it is your account first. The privacy policy has the details.

Accessibility

If something on Parity is hard to use with your setup, email us. We aim to reply within 5 working days and can send information in another format. See the accessibility statement.

6. How to complain to us

Please tell us first. Email contact@paritytrading.ai with "Complaint" in the subject, and include:

  • your name and the email address on your account, if you have one;
  • what happened, and when;
  • the page address, badge link or screenshot, if it helps;
  • what you would like us to do.

We aim to confirm we have your complaint within 5 working days, and to give a full answer within 30 days. Privacy requests follow the time limits in section 5. If we need longer, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer.

8. If Parity changes

Some planned features would bring new rules. Paid plans, advice tailored to one person, or real trading through a licensed broker would each require legal review before launch, and some would require registration. Real trading would only ever happen through a licensed broker that holds the money, under separate terms you would accept first. We will update this page, and its date, before any of that starts.
