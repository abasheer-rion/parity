Cookie policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

We use what Parity needs to work: a cookie that keeps you signed in, and a few settings saved in your browser. If you switch it on, we also use our own analytics, which keeps two random IDs in your browser. It is off unless you switch it on. Your analytics choice also decides whether page use can shape the order of what you see. We do not use advertising or any other company's tracking.

1. What cookies and browser storage are

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser and reads back later. Browser storage ("local storage") is similar, but the website's own code reads it, and it is never sent to our servers on its own.

5.1 Analytics

Parity's analytics is our own. No other company's script or service runs it, and it sets no cookie. It is off until you choose "Allow analytics" or switch it on in the cookie settings. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, it stays off and we do not offer it.

When it is on, each page you open in Parity records:

  • which page you opened, as a pattern such as "a strategy page". IDs, search words and anything after a question mark in the address are left out;
  • how long the page was on screen in front of you, and how far down you scrolled;
  • which buttons, links, tabs and other controls you clicked, by their short label such as "button: Run checks". Never what you type into a field;
  • quick repeated clicks on the same spot, which usually mean something did not respond;
  • error messages the page hit, with web addresses, email addresses and long numbers removed;
  • a few named actions, such as running a Verify check, making an account, placing a demo order or finishing a lesson, with small details such as the kind of file checked;
  • the two random IDs listed above, the app you were using, a rough screen size (phone, tablet or computer), your country as reported by our hosting network, and on the first page of a visit the name of the website that linked to us.

If you are signed in, we also store a scrambled code made from your account ID with a secret key, so that one person's visits on several devices count once. The code cannot be turned back into your account ID. We do not store your IP address (our server uses a shortened form for one minute to stop floods, then forgets it), your browser type, your email address or the text on the page. We do not combine analytics with data from other companies, and we do not use it for advertising.

We keep the single events for 13 months, then delete them. Daily totals with no IDs are kept for 25 months. Only named members of the Parity team can see the analytics. Switching analytics off stops it at once and deletes the two IDs from your browser. If you delete your account, we delete the events tied to it.

Page timing is not analytics and uses no cookie or storage: on about one page load in ten the page sends a few anonymous speed numbers, with no ID, which we add to hourly totals. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control turn it off. See the privacy policy.

5.2 Personalization

Personalization orders "What's new" and the home widgets from what you do in Parity. It sets no cookie. Its only browser item is parity.p.session, listed above. The profile itself is kept in our database, as the privacy policy explains.

Your analytics choice also decides what personalization may use. With analytics on, the pages you open and the controls you click can help order what you see. With analytics off, or if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, personalization uses only things Parity stores to make its features work, such as finished lessons and demo orders, and never your page use.

Personalization has its own switch in Settings, Personalization, where you can also see and reset your profile. Switching it off there does not change your analytics choice, and switching analytics off does not switch personalization off.
