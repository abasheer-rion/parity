Cookie policy

Draft for lawyer review Last updated 22 October 2026

We use only what Parity needs to work: a cookie that keeps you signed in, and a few settings saved in your browser. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

What cookies and browser storage are

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser and reads back later. Browser storage ("local storage") is similar, but the website's own code reads it, and it is never sent to our servers on its own.

Your choices

Because everything above is strictly necessary, it does not need your consent under the ePrivacy rules. We still show a notice so you know. You can open your cookie settings at any time:

You can also block or delete cookies and storage in your browser's settings. If you block the sign-in cookie, you will not be able to sign in.

We do not use analytics. If we ever add any, it will be off until you switch it on in the cookie settings, and this page will name the tool first.
