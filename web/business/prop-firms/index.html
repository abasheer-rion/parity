One verdict per account. The numbers behind it.

The screen uses the same checks as Parity Verify, judged for a record that lasts weeks, not years.

  • Holds up

    Makes money per trade after fees, no single bad run could erase it, and the result clears the luck check for the attempts behind it.

  • Likely luck

    Loses money per trade, could be wiped out by one bad run, or does not clear the luck check once its attempts are counted.

  • Not enough trades

    Fewer than 30 trades. Nothing can be told from so few, and a quick pass on a handful of large trades is itself worth a look.

  • Luck check

    The deflated Sharpe ratio of the trades, at the number of attempts the trader made.

  • Tries survived

    How many attempts the result could have taken and still pass the luck check.

  • Worst fall

    The largest drop from a high, and whether position size grew after losses.

  • Consistency

    How much of the profit came from the best day and from the best 5 trades.
