# New accounts get a short full-screen intro that ends on a real Verify result

## In plain words

The first time a new account opens Home, a full-screen intro asks three questions, one per screen:

1. What brings you to Parity? Check a strategy or a bot, learn how trading works, practise with a demo account, or read the research.
2. How much have you traded? Never, a little, or for years.
3. Which markets do you follow? Stocks, currencies, crypto, commodities. Pick any.

The last screen runs Verify on a well-known tested strategy for the market picked first (the ten-month trend on US stocks, or currency carry) and shows its verdict, its chart against the US stock market from the signature chart system ([DEC-0067](0067-one-signature-chart-system-for-lessons-strategy-pages-and-clips.md)), tests passed, Sharpe ratio and worst fall. "Go to Home" lands on Home with one card for the next thing to do and the getting started ring at the top right.

Every screen can be skipped. Escape or "Skip intro" ends it. A closed tab comes back on the same screen. Once finished or skipped it never opens again on any device.

The answers change what comes first: the getting started checklist and Home's widget order follow the goal (someone who has never traded also gets Academy early), and What's new ranks the markets and kind of item they named higher until their own use outweighs it.

Watchlist, Verify, the demo account's Positions in Trade and Academy's Progress page now say what to do when they are empty, with one button.

## Context

- The owner asked for a soft first-run onboarding like trading apps have, with smooth fades between steps, and pages that make it obvious where to go next.
- The pieces already existed: a three-step welcome after sign-up ([DEC-0044](0044-one-sign-in-panel-that-fades-in-over-the-front-page.md)) that kept its picks in one browser only, the checklist and tours ([DEC-0060](0060-a-getting-started-checklist-and-first-run-tours-and-no-bank-details-until-a-broker-partner.md)), personalization ([DEC-0068](0068-personalization-learns-from-use-ranks-what-s-new-and-runs-experiments-with-an-off-switch.md)) and Home's widgets ([DEC-0075](0075-the-app-home-is-a-full-width-grid-of-widgets-each-person-arranges.md)).

## Decision

- **One place for the questions.** The sign-up welcome is now one screen that says the account is ready and goes on. The questions moved into the app, where the answers are saved with the account.
- **Stored in `onboarding_state`.** Migration `20261114000000_onboarding_intro.sql` adds `intro_status`, `intro_step`, `intro_goal`, `intro_level` and `intro_markets` to the table that already holds the checklist and tours, so its row rules apply unchanged: own row only, and connected AI apps cannot write it. Check constraints limit each column to the listed answers. The guard keeps `intro_status` once set, so a stale tab cannot reopen the intro. `supabase/tests/test_onboarding.sql` covers both.
- **Who sees it.** A signed-in account whose row is saved in the database, that has not ended the intro and has not turned tours off. Accounts that used the app before the intro existed (a tour seen or a checklist item done) do not get it. The local preview and a failed database read do not open it by themselves, because "never again" could not be kept; `#/?intro=1` opens it anywhere.
- **Answers feed what already exists.** The goal maps onto the checklist's existing picks (`introRules.ts` `picksFor`). What's new mixes the answers into the profile it ranks with, weighted by 1 minus engagement, only while personalization is on; the reason line says "You said you follow currencies." Nothing new is stored server-side for personalization.
- **Motion.** Each screen rises and sharpens in from a small blur and leaves the same way, using the motion tokens. With reduced motion they only crossfade.
- **Accessibility.** The intro is a modal dialog: the app behind is inert, Tab stays inside, each new question takes focus, focus rings show for the keyboard only, and axe runs on every screen in `e2e/intro.spec.ts`.

## Consequences

- New accounts see Home only after the intro or a skip, and the Home tour is marked seen when the intro is finished, so Home does not open a second walk-through.
- Primary actions in the intro, the next step card and the empty states use the Invicta blue `accent` button variant.
- To show the intro again for an account, clear `intro_status` and `intro_step` on its row with the service role. People cannot clear it themselves.
