# Markets on the product side, crypto, perps, ETFs and what is new

## In plain words

The owner wants the product to cover "all the hot stuff": crypto, perps, ETFs and new financial hype, so people can find the next ETFs and coins. It should be free to research and, one day, free to trade. We added a Markets area to the product app at `/app/#/markets`. It shows crypto prices, perpetual futures, every US-listed ETF by theme, what is newly filed or trending, a page of hype terms explained, and a Practice mode with pretend money. It never says "buy", never ranks by gains, and every item has a sceptical "what to check" list.

## Context

[INT-0002](../intent/0002-two-products-a-research-and-simulation-platform-and-a-public-trading-app.md) rules out trading real money or advising individuals until the legal work is done. [DEC-0015](0015-two-sides-a-product-app-and-a-research-app-kept-apart.md) says the product presents evidence, ranks by evidence and not by return, and must not hype. Hype is exactly what this request is about, so the pages have to show new things honestly.

Most crypto exchanges forbid showing their data on someone else's site. We read each provider's terms on 22 October 2026 before choosing ([REF-0014](../reference/0014-markets-data-crypto-perps-and-etf-sources-and-their-terms.md)).

## Decision

1. **Sources.** All free, no key, all optional:
   - CoinGecko for crypto prices, one year of daily history and trending coins, with its attribution next to the data;
   - Hyperliquid's public API for perp funding, open interest and leverage;
   - SEC EDGAR for the ETF universe and new fund filings.
   - Turned down because their terms forbid display: Coinbase, Kraken, Deribit, OKX, Binance and Nasdaq's symbol file.
2. **Storage follows each source's terms.** CoinGecko data is overwritten on every fetch, because its terms discourage storing data. Perp and SEC data are kept with dates. Code is `engine/src/parity/markets/`; commands are `parity markets fetch`, `status` and `export`.
3. **Pages.** Markets has seven views: Overview, New and trending, Crypto, Perps, ETFs, Hype terms and Practice. They are wired into the product menu, its routes and the page list.
4. **No hype rules.**
   - Lists are ordered by size (market value, open interest) or by date, never by gains or popularity.
   - Trending coins are labelled as attention, not evidence.
   - Every coin, fund and filing has a "what to check" list: fees, liquidity, concentration and history.
   - Each page has a plain risk note. The Perps page shows how small a move wipes out a leveraged deposit.
   - The words "buy" and "sell" are never used as advice.
5. **Hype terms.** 21 new glossary entries in a new group, "New products and hype terms", from perps to prediction markets. Each has simple, analogy and precise text with a source, plus a new "what can go wrong" line. The glossary page and `docs/reference/0004-glossary.md` show that line too.
6. **Practice, not trading.** Practice positions are pretend, kept in the browser only, and labelled "No real money" everywhere. ETF prices are typed by the user, because no free ETF price feed we checked allows display. Real trading is a separate, future decision ([DEC-0019](0019-real-trading-later-what-it-would-legally-take.md)).
7. **Production.** The hourly News job also runs `parity markets fetch` (it skips sources fetched recently), and the deploy job runs `parity markets export dist/data`. The site reads `/data/markets/*.json`; local development reads `/local/markets/` from `engine/output/markets`.

## Consequences

- **Owner call: Hyperliquid display.** Its terms say nothing about data reuse, but its app is closed to US persons. The perps data is marked "ask". Deploys show it today; `parity markets export --no-ask` hides it with one flag. The owner should decide, ideally with a lawyer.
- **SEC contact.** SEC sources need the `PARITY_CONTACT` repository variable, an email address. A plain project URL was refused. Without it the ETF and filings views stay empty and the rest still works.
- **Short history.** Without a CoinGecko key, crypto history stops at one year, and new coin listings are paid only. A free Demo key adds rate limits in writing; a paid plan adds history.
- **ETF themes come from fund names.** This is rough. Some funds are missed or misfiled, and the page says so.
- **The wall holds.** Markets data is product-side only. Research never reads it, and nothing here becomes a research result without the usual tests.
