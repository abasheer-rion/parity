# Verify's imports are tested on real exports

## In plain words

Every broker and platform file Verify says it reads is now tested on a real export, or, where no real file could be published, on a copy of a real file's layout with made-up numbers. Testing on real files found and fixed real mistakes: commission rebates counted as costs, fees taken off twice, files in several currencies added up as if they were one, open trades counted as closed, and whole formats that were not recognised at all. Verify now also reads MetaTrader 4 and 5 reports as they are saved (HTML in any language, and the Excel "Open XML" file), TradingView's Excel download, Myfxbook, Coinbase's transaction report, Binance's account statement and Tradovate's position history and orders. When a row cannot be read, the page says which line and which cell.

## Context

[DEC-0081](0081-verify-is-a-public-page-with-broker-imports-embeds-and-a-citable-method.md) added 11 broker and exchange layouts, all written from help pages and marked "unverified layout". On 3 November 2026 the owner asked for imports that work for a real trader. MetaTrader 4 and 5 statements and TradingView's Strategy Tester download are what most retail currency and prop-firm traders have, and Verify read neither as saved.

## Decision

1. **Real files in the tests.** Excerpts of real exports published under MIT, Apache-2.0, BSL-1.0 or the Unlicense are in `web/tests/fixtures/verify/real`, with their source, commit, licence and every change in `SOURCES.md`; personal details were replaced. Sources include [Interactive Brokers Flex](https://github.com/agusalex/tax_calculator), [MetaTrader 4 statements](https://github.com/codecat92/apex-grid-ea), [MetaTrader 5 reports](https://github.com/PopovMP/mt-report-parser), [TradingView exports](https://github.com/pineforge-4pass/pineforge-corpus), [Coinbase reports](https://github.com/Delta-Compute/DeltaCFOAgent) and [Alpaca's own client](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py). Where real files could be seen but not republished, `web/tests/fixtures/verify/layout` copies their layout with made-up numbers. `web/tests/verify/real-exports.test.ts` checks the trades read from each against numbers worked out by hand, and `web/e2e/verify-imports.spec.ts` uploads every one on `/verify/`.
2. **Three levels, shown on the page.** Each supported file says "Tested on a real export", "Layout copied from a real export" or "Unverified layout" (`web/src/lib/verify/parse/supported.ts`). Only Bybit's two layouts are still unverified.
3. **Files as they are saved.** MetaTrader reports are found by the shape of their rows, not their header words, so any terminal language works; UTF-16 files are decoded. Excel files are unzipped in the browser with no library. A JSON list of Alpaca activities is read as a table.
4. **Money in one currency.** With a rate per row (Interactive Brokers' FXRateToBase) every amount becomes the base currency. A file with several currencies and no rates says so plainly.
5. **Times.** A time zone written in a cell or a header ("Date(UTC+1)", "Closing Time (UTC-7)", "UTC", "EST") is turned into UTC. MetaTrader's server time and Interactive Brokers' US Eastern time are stated, not guessed.
6. **Fees, as each platform means them.** Costs and rebates by sign where the platform uses one (Interactive Brokers), realized profit used as already net or before fees per platform, profit already net of commission not reduced again (Myfxbook, NinjaTrader), fees in a coin we cannot price named.
7. **Plain errors.** A skipped row is named with its line and the cell: "Line 3: the close date "2024-13-45" is not a date we can read." Files that are not trade lists (a Kraken ledger, a Binance futures transaction history) say what to export instead.

## Consequences

- A trader can upload the file their platform gives them, in the form it gives it, and see what was read before anything is checked.
- New layouts need a real export (or a copied layout) and a test before the "unverified" label comes off.
- The methodology page and [REF-0030](../reference/0030-parity-verify-methodology.md) list the imports and how fills, fees, currencies and times are handled.
