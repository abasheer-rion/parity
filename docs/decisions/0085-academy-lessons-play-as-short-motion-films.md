# Academy lessons play as short motion films

## In plain words

Each lesson in "Reading a backtest" and "Luck vs skill" now has a film of 35 to 50 seconds instead of a step player with Back and Next. Lines draw, the big number counts, the camera moves in and out, words fill the screen, and the shot cuts with a quick blur, the way short finance explainers on TikTok look. It plays like a video: play and pause, a position bar with chapters, speed, captions and loop. The lesson text stays beside it.

The Motion setting now has Off as well as Full and Reduced, in Settings and on the film itself. With Reduced or Off nothing plays by itself: the film shows a still picture with a play button, and pressing it plays the same film as an MP4 with the browser's own controls and captions.

The same films render as 9:16 and 1:1 MP4 clips for social with `npm run content -- --reels`.

## Context

- The owner's feedback of 28 October 2026 on the step player: "I want actual edits, Claude in motion not steps, this looks half baked", with a TikTok of the look he wants (an opening range breakout test by quantlabs: huge words, a thin chart drawing, counting numbers, cuts every few seconds, captions).
- His Mac has Reduce Motion on, so every lesson showed still frames with a note. A person who asks for less motion should still be able to watch the film when they choose to (WCAG 2.2.2 and 2.3.3 allow user-started motion with a pause control).
- The signature chart system ([DEC-0067](0067-one-signature-chart-system-for-lessons-strategy-pages-and-clips.md)) already draws each lesson's chart at any position between its steps, in the app and in Node.

## Decision

1. **A lesson can carry a `reel`:** a list of beats in its course file. Each beat is one spoken line (`say`, starred words light up), an optional big line at the top (`head`, which can count or follow the chart's line), a shot (`chart` with a step position `s` and a camera `cam`, `statement`, `grid` of small paths or `panels` side by side) and an optional `chapter`. The chart is the lesson's own `visual`, so the film and the public page show the same numbers.
2. **One clock.** `web/src/ui/reel/timeline.ts` times the beats from the words (about 2.6 words a second), carries the chart and camera from beat to beat, and splits each line into caption cards with a time per word. `web/src/ui/reel/render.ts` draws any moment as SVG. A new shot is a 0.4 second cut with a sideways blur. The film opens from black and closes to black, so it loops cleanly.
3. **The player** (`web/src/ui/reel/ReelPlayer.tsx`) plays by itself when it scrolls into view, with Full motion and the lesson autoplay setting on, loops, and pauses off screen or in a hidden tab. Controls: play and pause, a position bar with chapter marks, time, speed (0.5x to 2x), captions (C), loop, and the Motion choice. Space or K plays and pauses, J and L move 10 seconds. A transcript lists every line with its time, and "Show the numbers" opens the table behind the chart.
4. **Reduced motion and Off** show the poster frame (the last whole-chart moment) and a play button. Pressing it plays `web/public/content/academy/<course>-<lesson>.mp4` with its WebVTT captions. Lessons without a film keep the step player.
5. **Off is a fourth Motion choice** ([REF-0023](../reference/0023-motion-system.md)): written as `data-motion="reduced"` plus `data-motion-off`, so every reduced-motion rule applies and the fades stop too.
6. **Rendering.** `npm run content -- --reels` writes the 9:16 and 1:1 clips with burned-in captions and the risk line, a caption file and a voice-over script per film to `~/Desktop/parity-clips/academy-motion/`, and the 16:9 film with its caption file to `web/public/content/academy/`. `academy/reels.json` records a hash of each film's beats and chart; a test fails when a lesson changes and its film was not rendered again.

## Consequences

- The first ten films are in the repository, about 0.7 MB each.
- A change to a lesson's reel or chart needs a new render of that film before the tests pass.
- A change to how films are drawn (the renderer) does not change the hash; render the films again by hand when the look changes.
- Every frame names what its numbers are (hypothetical, simulated or a real run) at the top. Clips carry the risk line.
- Files: `web/src/ui/reel/`, `web/src/academy/AcademyApp.tsx`, `web/src/lib/academy.ts`, `web/src/ui/motionCore.ts`, `web/scripts/content/reels.ts`, `web/scripts/content/render.ts`, `academy/reels.json`, tests in `web/tests/reel.test.ts`, `web/tests/reelPlayer.test.tsx`, `web/tests/motion.test.tsx` and `web/e2e/academy.spec.ts`.
