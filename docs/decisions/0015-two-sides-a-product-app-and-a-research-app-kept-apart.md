# Two sides, a product app and a research app, kept apart

## In plain words

Parity's site now has two separate sides:

- **The product app, at `/app/`.** It is for anyone. Like Public, it is easy to find, follow and understand strategies. Like QuantPad, it has tools to test ideas.
- **The research app, at `/research/`.** This is where results are made.

Each side has its own dashboard, its own menu and its own pages. A page explains both sides and the wall between them. The public front page offers one door to each. The owner asked for this so that the product cannot bias the research ([INT-0002](../intent/0002-two-products-a-research-and-simulation-platform-and-a-public-trading-app.md)).

## Context

Until now, one dashboard mixed everything. A "Markets" and "Research" switch sat in the same menu, and Sim World was the home page.

A product built to attract people creates pressure to show winners. A research project needs the opposite: count every try and report the failures. Mixing the two makes it easy for the first pressure to leak into the second, for example by ranking strategies by return or by promoting search leads as results.

## Decision

1. **Two apps, one code base.**
   - `web/app/index.html` is the product and `web/research/index.html` is the research.
   - Both load the same code, and `<html data-space>` tells it which side it is (`web/src/lib/space.ts`).
   - The sign-in check protects both (`functions/_middleware.ts`, `public/_routes.json`).
2. **Every page belongs to one side, or to both** (`web/src/lib/pages.ts`). A link to a page on the other side opens that side.
   - **Product:** Home, Discover, Watchlist, Compare, Try settings, What if, News and Biggest days.
   - **Research:** Research home, Research docs, Bibliography, Official record, Strategy search, Algorithm library, Early signals, Raw prices, Sim World, Data health and Data sources.
   - **Both:** Start here, How Parity works, Glossary and Settings.
3. **The product dashboard** works like Public's home screen:
   - a box to ask questions in plain words;
   - a watchlist;
   - "worth a look", ranked by how well each strategy held up in testing, never by return;
   - browsing by theme;
   - "what if";
   - this year's leaders and laggards;
   - a placeholder for building a strategy by describing it, the QuantPad-like helper that comes next.
4. **The research dashboard** shows:
   - the question and today's answer;
   - verdicts across all strategies;
   - the strategies closest to an answer;
   - the official record;
   - the always-on search ([DEC-0014](0014-a-strategy-factory-and-an-always-on-search.md));
   - open hypotheses;
   - recent decisions;
   - data health.
5. **The wall.** These rules are written on the "How Parity works" page and on the front page:
   1. Research decides and the product presents. Every number in the product comes from research, with its verdict.
   2. Research never sees product numbers. Sign-ups, watchlists, clicks and popularity are not shown there and never feed a test.
   3. Search leads stay on the research side until they are registered and run officially.
   4. The product ranks by evidence, not by return.
   5. Every try is counted.
6. **The front page** (`web/index.html`) has:
   - a hero with a labelled example card;
   - two doors, the app and research;
   - the wall in three rules;
   - how an idea becomes a result.

## Alternatives considered

- **Separate subdomains now** (`app.` and `research.`). Not yet. Two paths on one domain share one sign-in and one deploy. INT-0002 still proposes `sim.endyland.com` for the world simulator when it becomes its own product.
- **Separate code bases.** Rejected for now. It would double the UI library and the build. The wall lives in which data each side shows, not in the repository.

## Consequences

- Sim World moved from the home page to `#/sim` on the research side.
- Old bookmarks to `/app/#/docs` and similar still work, because they redirect to the research side.
- Spot natural gas spikes make "Commodity trend" show -88.6% for 2026 on the product home ([DEC-0014](0014-a-strategy-factory-and-an-always-on-search.md) consequences). The product shows research as it is. The fix belongs to research.
