# Every Verify check ends in one report that can be shared and printed

## In plain words

Whatever is checked, a trade file, strategy code, one of our strategies or a seller's claim, the answer is now the same report. It opens with the verdict in one sentence and the numbers that decided it, then what the seller claims, five pictures of how the record behaves, the five checks, what was checked and what could not be. Every number explains itself on hover or tap. The pictures play once as they come into view, or stand still under the Motion setting. A report can be shared as a link, saved as a PDF and turned into an image for a post. Parity stores none of it: the report travels inside the link.

## Context

Verify's result was five cards ([DEC-0081](0081-verify-is-a-public-page-with-broker-imports-embeds-and-a-citable-method.md)) and, in the signed-in app only, the "how it holds up" section ([DEC-0067](0067-one-signature-chart-system-for-lessons-strategy-pages-and-clips.md)). On the public page there were no pictures at all and nothing could be shared without publishing a badge. On 3 November 2026 the owner asked for "the actual product".

## Decision

1. **One shape** (`web/src/lib/verify/report/model.ts`, version 1): built from any check's result, it holds the verdict, the cards, the pictures' numbers (a growth curve thinned to 240 points keeping each stretch's highs and lows, the spread under reshuffling, groups), the claims with their words, the inputs used, what could not be checked and the method version. The page, a shared link, the PDF and the image card all draw from it.
2. **Five pictures** on the signature charts: growth (against the S&P 500 from our daily prices when signed in, otherwise in its own terms, and in money without a balance), the luck check at 1 to 1 billion tries with the tries counted marked, the spread of the Sharpe ratio (1,000 reshuffles in 21-day blocks) or of profit per trade (1,000 draws of the trades, seeded) with its 95% range, falls from a high, and where it wins and loses (calm and volatile markets and crises with the S&P 500, each year, each market, long and short). A missing picture says why in its place. Pictures draw when they come near the screen, so a long report opens fast on a phone, and open on the step that carries the verdict when motion is reduced.
3. **A link holds the whole report** (`link.ts`): deflated JSON after `#r=` in `/verify/report/`, usually 4 to 8 KB. Browsers never send the part after "#", so Parity stores nothing, and the page says that Parity cannot vouch that a shared link was not changed. Every field is checked for type and size before anything is drawn; a damaged link says so plainly.
4. **Save as PDF** prints the report on a light page with each picture as a still of its last step and the controls hidden; every browser's print dialog saves a PDF.
5. **An image card**, 1200 by 630 like link previews: the verdict, how many tries it survives, what was checked, the sentence and a small growth line, drawn in the browser with the Inter font embedded, downloaded as a PNG or handed to the phone's share sheet.
6. **A published badge** shows the same report from its summary numbers, and says its pictures are not kept.
7. **Look and access**: the front page's look (no top bar, big solid cards, Invicta blue for the main action), plain words, WCAG 2.2 AA ([DEC-0061](0061-every-part-meets-wcag-2-2-aa-and-ci-fails-on-overlap-or-clipped-numbers.md)): every picture has a description and its numbers as a table, every number its meaning.

## Alternatives considered

- **Store reports and share a short address.** Rejected for now: it would keep people's results on our servers, and a published badge already does that for those who want it.
- **Sign shared links.** Deferred: it needs a server key; the page says plainly that a link is not vouched for.
- **A PDF made on our server.** Rejected: the record would have to be sent to us. Printing in the browser gives the same file.

## Consequences

- The public page now shows the pictures that were only in the app, and every check can be shared without an account.
- Links are long (a few kilobytes). Some chat apps may cut them; the image card and the PDF are the fallbacks.
- Files: `web/src/lib/verify/report/`, `web/src/pages/verify/report/`, `web/src/pages/verify/Result.tsx`, `web/src/verify/ReportPage.tsx`, `web/verify/report/index.html`, tests in `web/tests/verify/report.test.tsx`, `web/tests/verify/report-link.test.ts` and `web/e2e/verify-report.spec.ts`.
