# Pairs and stat arb on 49 industries, with single stocks research only

## In plain words

We added pairs trading: betting that two prices that usually move together, like soft drinks and beer, close any gap that opens between them. We tested four ways of choosing pairs on 55 years of US industry data. None makes money after costs since 1999. The simplest one did earn something before costs, and that edge has shrunk decade by decade, just as the research said it would. Single company pairs such as Coca-Cola and PepsiCo are built and tested in code, but they need a stock data licence the owner has to switch on.

## Context

The owner asked for stock pairs (Pepsi and Coca-Cola and many more) and statistical arbitrage. [RES-0028](../research/0028-pairs-trading-and-statistical-arbitrage.md) covers the research and its pitfalls. Free single-stock daily prices with terms that allow programs are scarce: Yahoo and Stooq forbid scripts ([DEC-0008](0008-benchmark-data-without-yahoo.md)), Nasdaq Data Link needs an account. The team already holds a free Alpaca plan, whose terms allow the account holder's own use only.

## Decision

- **Data.** French's 49 daily US industry portfolios (public licence, from 1926) are the long test bed, `ind49.*`. Single stocks and ETFs come from Alpaca daily bars since 2016, `stk.*`, display rights `research` in the registry: values feed backtests and statistics, and only derived numbers (z-scores, hedge ratios, p-values, returns) reach any page. A guard refuses to write any price field to `pairs.json`.
- **Universe.** 25 hand-picked linked industry pairs and every same-sector industry pair for discovery. For stocks: KO/PEP, XOM/CVX, V/MA, HD/LOW, MCD/YUM, UPS/FDX, GM/F, JPM/BAC, WMT/TGT, BHP/RIO, GLD/GDX, GOOGL/GOOG, BRK.A/BRK.B, sector funds against their top holdings, and nine sectors of today's large companies for discovery (survivorship flagged).
- **Methods.** Distance, rolling Engle-Granger cointegration with a half-life filter, Kalman hedge ratios and PCA residuals (Avellaneda and Lee). Pairs are chosen every six months from the past year only. Entry at a z-score, exit near zero, a stop-out at four (a broken pair is not reopened that window) and a time stop at three half-lives.
- **Costs.** Industry baskets pay at least 10 basis points a trade and 50 a year to borrow the short leg; stocks 5 and 30. Cost sensitivity runs from 0 to 40 basis points and 0 to 200 a year.
- **Honesty.** Look-ahead truncation tests on prices that really are linked, for all four methods. Every recipe runs the eleven gauntlet tests; the deflated Sharpe counts every grid variation and every pairs draw in the always-on search, which now has a pairs space capped at 15% of each batch.
- **Pages.** A Pairs page in research and a Pairs view in Workbench read `pairs.json` from `parity pairs report`.

## Results (1999 to October 2026, after costs)

| Recipe | Sharpe | Chance not luck | Tests passed | Before costs, 1970s to 2010s | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Industry pairs, closest prices | 0.00 | 0% | 1 of 11 | 0.35, 0.51, 0.41, 0.22 | luck |
| Linked industries, cointegrated | -0.08 | 0% | 1 of 11 | 0.20, 0.08, 0.12, 0.04 | fails |
| Linked industries, moving hedge | -0.31 | 0% | 1 of 11 | 0.57, 0.37, 0.01, 0.08 | fails |
| Industry pairs, cointegrated | -0.57 | 0% | 0 of 11 | below zero throughout | fails |
| Industry leftovers (PCA) | -1.00 | 0% | 0 of 11 | below zero before 1999 | fails |

- **Luck check.** 22 variations counted so far (each recipe's settings grid); the search adds its pairs draws as it runs. The deflated Sharpe is 0% for all five.
- **Decay.** The distance rule's edge before costs fell from about 0.5 in 1985 to 1998 to about 0.2 since 2009; the Kalman pairs from 0.57 in 1970 to 1984 to near zero. Costs take what is left.
- **More pairs, worse.** Letting cointegration choose from every same-sector pair loses more than the 25 hand-picked ones: testing many pairs finds links that were luck.
- **Industries reverse less than stocks.** PCA leftovers lose before costs for industries before 1999; whole industries tend to keep moving rather than snap back.

## Consequences

- Nothing here beats the market or passes the luck check. The recipes stay in the nightly run and the search keeps drawing pairs within its budget.
- Owner decision: allow the team Alpaca key on the research machine (`ALPACA_KEY_ID`, `ALPACA_SECRET_KEY`) so `parity fetch` gets stock bars and the stock recipes run. Ten years of stocks is short; a longer licensed history (Tiingo or EODHD, REF-0015) is a paid choice.
