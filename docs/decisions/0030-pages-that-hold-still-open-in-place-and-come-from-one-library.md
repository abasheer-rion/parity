# Pages that hold still, open in place and come from one library

## In plain words

The app should feel calm. Nothing on a page should jump while it loads, while its numbers arrive, when you hover over something or when you open a row. Things that open should grow out of where they are, and things that float (menus, popovers, dialogs, drawers) should come out of the button that opened them and go back into it. Every one of these behaviours is now a part in the UI library, with a live demo in the gallery, so each page uses the same part instead of its own version.

We measure the jumping with the browser's own score, cumulative layout shift. Every page has to stay under 0.02, at desktop and phone width, and a script checks it.

## Context

The library (REF-0009) already set colours, type, buttons, tables and charts, and DEC-0017 set the motion rules. What it did not have was a shared answer for loading, opening and floating. Pages showed a line of "Loading" text and then replaced it, opened details with their own code, kept long lists to 100 rows a page, and positioned menus once without following the page. The owner asked for a library that feels modern and anchored, where nothing jitters.

A first measurement on a fast local network found almost no layout shift (worst page 0.018, the glossary). The risk was in what a fast machine hides: late data, font swaps, scroll bars appearing, and the hand-made patterns each page used.

## Decision

1. **One part for each pattern.** New parts in `web/src/ui`: `Collapse`, `Disclosure` and `Accordion` for opening in place; `Popover` and a rebuilt `Drawer`, `Dialog`, `Menu`, `Tooltip` and toasts that are anchored and animate out as well as in; `Skeleton` variants sized like the real content and `PageSkeleton` for whole pages; `VirtualList` and a `DataTable` that can stick its header, draw only the visible rows of thousands, open rows in place and glide rows on a sort; `AnimatedNumber` and `Stagger`; `Tabs` with a sliding underline and `Segmented` with a sliding thumb; `useResource` for data that shows its last copy at once.
2. **Rules that stop jumping**, set once in the library: fallback fonts stretched to the web fonts' measurements, `scrollbar-gutter: stable`, charts that read their width before the first paint, sort arrows that keep their space, a loading button that keeps its width, hover that never changes size, and animation by transform and opacity only. `Collapse` is the one height animation, and it grows from its own top edge on purpose.
3. **Motion stays inside DEC-0017.** Entrances under 400ms, a stagger capped at 240ms, nothing that loops, and nothing moves for people who ask for reduced motion.
4. **A measurement that can fail.** `npm run cls` loads every page of the app, the research side and the terminal at 1440 and 390 pixels, waits for the data, hovers and scrolls, and fails if any page reaches 0.02. `CLS_SLOW=1` adds a slow network and late fonts.

## Consequences

- Pages adopt the parts in place of one-off markup and CSS, and the replaced CSS is removed.
- The strategy search can show all of its rows in one smooth list instead of pages of 100.
- A page that reaches 0.02 is a bug, and the script names the element that moved.
- Floating things stay on screen and follow their trigger; dialogs and drawers lock the page without shifting it.

## Measured

Worst layout shift across 80 page views (40 pages at 1440 and 390 pixels), measured on 23 October 2026:

| Network | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fast (local) | 0.018 (glossary) | 0.002 |
| Slow, fonts late (`CLS_SLOW=1`) | 0.085 (research home on a phone), 8 pages at or over 0.02 | 0.018, none at or over 0.02 |
