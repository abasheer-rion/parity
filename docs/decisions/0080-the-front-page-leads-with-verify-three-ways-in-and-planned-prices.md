# The front page leads with Verify, three ways in and planned prices

## In plain words

On 27 October the owner cut Parity's scope to Verify. The front page now says one thing first: "AI-native verification platform for retail traders of all sizes." It has one main button, "Verify a strategy or trade record", and everything else on the page backs that up. The rest of Parity (Trade, Sim World, Academy, Workbench, Terminal, research, markets) is still there, in a quieter list near the bottom.

## Context

[DEC-0079](0079-parity-is-an-ai-native-verification-platform-for-retail-traders-of-all-sizes.md) records the new positioning: Verify checks algos, trades, strategies and claims, through the website, the API or an AI connector (MCP). The front page from [DEC-0039](0039-a-front-page-for-verify-dark-glass-and-scroll-scenes.md) already put Verify first, but it also gave large sections to the markets we cover and to how our own strategies are tested, and its main button went into the signed-in app.

## Decision

The page, top to bottom:

1. **Hero.** The tagline as the heading, a one-line explanation, the main button to `/verify/` and a quiet link to the methodology at `/methodology/`. The moving field of paths stays behind it.
2. **Three ways in.** Three large cards: on the web (upload a trade file or describe a bot), through the API (`/developers/`, with a real `curl` line) and from your AI (`/connect/`, with the tools `verify_trades`, `verify_strategy` and `verify_claim`).
3. **The two scenes** from DEC-0039: Bot Battle's 99.1% win rate that still loses money, and a Verify check with its verdict.
4. **Proof.** The counted tries, strategies tested and how many beat the market, read from the same live counts as before. The five verdicts in one sentence each. An example badge, drawn like the real one and marked "Example".
5. **Pricing**, from [DEC-0032](0032-parity-verify-as-a-data-and-api-business-with-a-free-retail-tier.md): Free (5 checks a month), Pro $15 a month, Seller $49 a month, API from $500 a month, Platform from $2,000 a month, Data licence from $25,000 a year. The page says checking is free today, paid plans are not on sale and there is no checkout.
6. **For businesses**: prop firms, copy-trading platforms, bot and signal marketplaces and brokers. A score on every listing through the API, screening funded traders, badges, and webhooks later. One button emails contact@paritytrading.app with the subject filled in.
7. **Explore Parity**: small links to the rest of the product.

Also:

- **The corner nav** reads Verify, Developers, Connect AI, then Log in and Sign up. Phones keep Verify, Log in and Sign up.
- **In the signed-in app**, Verify is the first item in the product sidebar, with an accent icon. No page was removed.
- **The main button is Invicta blue** on every page that uses the shared site kit (front page, sign-in pages, 404), with white text at 4.7:1.
- **Title, description and share card** for `/` now use the tagline. The organisation's email is contact@paritytrading.app.
- **Removed** from the front page: the large markets list and the three looping "how our strategies are tested" demos. Research still has both.

## Consequences

- `/verify/` and `/methodology/` are built in sibling changes. Until they ship, those links lead to whatever those addresses serve.
- The screenshot baselines for the front page, the sign-in pages and the app pages with a sidebar change.
- Prices on the page are the starting points from DEC-0032, not commitments. Charging needs its own decision.
