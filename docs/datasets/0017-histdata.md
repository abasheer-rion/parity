# HistData.com

## In plain words

A free website with minute by minute currency prices going back many years, packed one month per file.

## Summary

Free tick and 1-minute bar data for over 80 FX pairs plus a few commodities and stock indexes, in ASCII, MetaTrader, NinjaTrader and Excel formats.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Free with no warranty; the FAQ says use the data at your own risk. No redistribution terms stated. Personal research use only.

**Quality:** All timestamps are Eastern Standard Time with no daylight saving shift, which must be converted. Source of the quotes is not stated, and gaps are common.
