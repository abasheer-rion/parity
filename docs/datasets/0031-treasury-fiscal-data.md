# US Treasury Fiscal Data API

## In plain words

The US government's own record of how much it owes, spends and pays in interest.

## Summary

US government debt, spending, receipts, average interest rates on Treasury debt and Treasury exchange rates used for reporting.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Free and without restriction, including commercial use and redistribution (source: https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/api-documentation/).

**Quality:** Every field comes back as a quoted string, so numbers must be converted. Daily yield curves are easier to get from FRED.
