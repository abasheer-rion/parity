# FRED and ALFRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis)

## In plain words

A giant free library of economic numbers run by a US central bank office. It is where we get our currency prices, interest rates and oil prices.

## Summary

Hundreds of thousands of US and international series: exchange rates, interest rates, inflation, jobs, oil and gas prices, and the VIX. ALFRED keeps every past version of a series, so you can see what a number looked like on the day it came out.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Free to use under the FRED API terms. Apps must say they use the FRED API and are not endorsed by the St. Louis Fed. Some series belong to other owners (for example S&P and CBOE) and are marked Copyright; personal use is fine but other use needs the owner's permission. https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/terms_of_use.html

**Quality:** H.10 FX rates are noon New York buying rates, not closing prices. The OECD interbank rates for the euro area and UK currently stop at 2026-01. SP500 only covers the last 10 years and has no dividends.
