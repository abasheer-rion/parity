# Alpha Vantage

## In plain words

A company with a free but very small allowance of price and news data each day.

## Summary

Daily, weekly and monthly FX bars, US economic indicators, and NEWS_SENTIMENT with sentiment scores that can be filtered by currency (for example FOREX:USD). Intraday FX is premium.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Use under the Alpha Vantage terms of service, accepted when getting a key. https://www.alphavantage.co/terms_of_service/

**Quality:** The 25 a day cap makes backfills slow. Economic indicators are actual values only, no forecasts. The source of FX quotes is not stated.
