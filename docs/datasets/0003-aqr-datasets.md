# AQR Data Sets

## In plain words

A big investment firm posts the results of its research, including how simple currency strategies would have done over many years.

## Summary

Factor return series from AQR papers. Time Series Momentum covers 58 futures and forwards including currencies; Century of Factor Premia has value, momentum, carry and defensive returns across six asset classes; Value and Momentum Everywhere includes currencies.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** AQR's site terms forbid reproducing, distributing or publishing any information from the site without written consent, with no carve out for personal use. Use for private comparison only. https://www.aqr.com/Terms-of-Use

**Quality:** Series are rebuilt and updated on different schedules (TSMOM last updated 2026-05-29, Century of Factor Premia 2026-02-27). Returns are AQR's own constructions and gross of real costs.
