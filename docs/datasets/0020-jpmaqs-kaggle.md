# Macrosynergy JPMaQS sample (Kaggle)

## In plain words

A free sample from a big bank's data product that records what was known about each country's economy on each day.

## Summary

A small sample of point in time macro indicators for 23 currency areas (growth, inflation, inflation targets, credit, real yields) plus FX forward, rates swap and equity future returns, carry and FX tradability flags.
