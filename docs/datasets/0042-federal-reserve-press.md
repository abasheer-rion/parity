# Federal Reserve press releases

## In plain words

The US central bank's own announcements, straight from its website.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Public domain US government work: material may be copied and distributed without permission; credit the Board. https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm

**Quality:** Many releases are routine bank approvals, not market news. The feed starts with a byte order mark, which our parser handles.
