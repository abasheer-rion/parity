# EIA Open Data API v2

## In plain words

The US government office that tracks oil and gas, including how much is stored each week.

## Summary

Oil and gas spot prices, weekly crude and gas inventories, production, the Short Term Energy Outlook and international energy data.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** US government data is public domain. EIA asks for a source line with the publication date. https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php

**Quality:** Without a key the API answers API_KEY_MISSING (tested). Weekly inventory numbers can be revised later and the API shows the latest values. WTI was negative on 2020-04-20.
