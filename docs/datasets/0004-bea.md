# BEA Data API

## In plain words

The US government office that adds up how big the economy is and how much people spend.

## Summary

US GDP, PCE inflation, personal income, and international trade and investment figures.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Free key after giving a name and email and agreeing to the terms of service. US government data, generally public domain. https://apps.bea.gov/api/signup/

**Quality:** GDP is revised several times after the first estimate and the API gives the latest values only. Use ALFRED if first estimates matter.
