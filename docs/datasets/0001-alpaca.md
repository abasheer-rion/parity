# Alpaca paper trading

## In plain words

A pretend stock account where a program can practice buying and selling with fake money.

## Summary

A free paper account open to anyone with an email, starting at 100,000 virtual dollars, for US stocks and crypto. No forex.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Paper only accounts may only use IEX market data. Standard Alpaca terms apply. https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/paper-trading

**Quality:** Fills ignore market impact, slippage, dividends and fees, and get random partial fills 10 percent of the time. FX is listed only as on the roadmap.
