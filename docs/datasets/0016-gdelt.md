# GDELT Project

## In plain words

A robot that reads news from around the world every 15 minutes and writes down what happened and whether the mood was good or bad.

## Summary

Worldwide news monitoring in 65 languages: events, themes, people, places and a tone score. The DOC 2.0 API gives article lists and tone timelines; raw files and Google BigQuery hold the long history.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** The whole database is 100 percent free and open; citation of GDELT is expected. https://www.gdeltproject.org/data.html

**Quality:** Machine coded, so noisy, with duplicate stories and mis-tagged events. Tone is a simple word count measure. Coverage of sources grows over time, which can bias trends.
