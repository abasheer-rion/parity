# Databento CME FX futures (nanosecond)

## In plain words

Paid data where every order on the futures exchange is stamped to the billionth of a second.

## Summary

CME Globex market data (dataset GLBX.MDP3), including currency futures, at full order book depth (MBO) with nanosecond timestamps.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Paid licence. New users get "$125 in free credits that can be used on historical market data". Live data plans are "$199 per month" (Standard), "$1,750 per month" (Plus) and "$4,500 per month" (Unlimited). Timestamps are "the number of nanoseconds since the UNIX epoch". Exchange licence terms also apply.

**Quality:** Futures, not spot FX. The only realistic route we found to nanosecond FX data at a research budget. Not yet used.
