# BIS Triennial Survey FX turnover

## In plain words

Every three years central banks count how much currency trading happens in a month. This is that count: about 9.5 trillion US dollars a day in April 2025.

## Summary

Average daily FX turnover in April of each survey year, by currency and by instrument (spot, outright forwards, NDFs, FX swaps, currency swaps, options), in USD millions, from dataflow WS_DER_OTC_TOV.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** BIS terms: "if the statistics are reproduced, the BIS must be cited in your publication or product as the source"; use "must not be potentially misleading, for example by implying endorsement or affiliation with the BIS"; in a commercial product their inclusion must "not result in any additional charge". The APIs are offered "as-is" and the BIS "reserves the right to limit or suspend any User's IP address access to the APIs at any time". https://data.bis.org/help/legal

**Quality:** Net-net basis (each trade counted once). Shares add to 200% because each trade has two currencies. Survey years only, so nothing between surveys. Fetched with no key (tested 2026-10-21): total 7,468 billion USD a day for 2022 and 9,510 billion for 2025.
