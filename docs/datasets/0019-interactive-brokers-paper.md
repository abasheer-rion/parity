# Interactive Brokers paper trading

## In plain words

A big real broker lets its customers practice with fake money using real prices, including currencies.

## Summary

A paper account requested from inside a live IBKR account, with the same permissions as the live account, prices from real market conditions, and access through TWS or IB Gateway. The separate free trial has delayed data except spot FX and metals, which are live.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Standard IBKR account terms. Market data is licensed per subscription and cannot be redistributed.

**Quality:** Needs a full brokerage application first. Third parties say API market data needs live data permissions on a funded account, and that the free trial cannot use the API (unverified).
