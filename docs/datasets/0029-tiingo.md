# Tiingo

## In plain words

A data company with a free plan for daily stock prices, which could be a backup for our S&P 500 benchmark.

## Summary

End of day prices for over 110,000 securities on the free plan. Real time, crypto, news and fundamentals need paid plans, and forex is not in the free plan.
