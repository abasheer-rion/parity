# Finnhub

## In plain words

A data company whose free plan gives live market headlines, but currency prices and the economic calendar cost money.

## Summary

A free market news feed with a forex category. Forex candles, all forex rates and the economic calendar are marked Premium, and historical calendar surprises are Enterprise only.
