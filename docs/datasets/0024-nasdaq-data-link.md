# Nasdaq Data Link (formerly Quandl)

## In plain words

A data shop run by the Nasdaq exchange. Most of the good stuff costs money now.

## Summary

A marketplace of mostly paid datasets with a small set of free ones. The free continuous futures set (CHRIS) is deprecated and no longer updated.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Each dataset has its own license; free datasets carry their publisher's terms.

**Quality:** Many free datasets are stale or retired. What remains free is mostly also on FRED. CHRIS deprecation is from a third party citing Nasdaq support (unverified).
