# Binance public data (data.binance.vision)

## In plain words

The biggest crypto exchange posts free files of every past crypto price and trade.

## Summary

Daily and monthly zip files of spot and futures klines, trades and aggregate trades, plus futures funding rates, each with a SHA256 checksum.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Data is under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 per the Binance Vision Dataset Terms (updated 2026-08-26). Academic research and personal backtesting are allowed; commercial use, live trading use and paid signals are not. https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md

**Quality:** Spot timestamps switch from milliseconds to microseconds from 2025-01-01. Binance sometimes replaces archived files; the repo logs these corrections. Crypto only.
