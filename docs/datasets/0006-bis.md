# BIS Data Portal (SDMX API)

## In plain words

The central banks' own club keeps a list of every country's main interest rate. We can download it all in one place.

## Summary

Central bank policy rates for many countries in one dataset, plus effective exchange rates, credit and banking statistics.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Free to reproduce if the BIS is cited as the source, the use is not misleading and does not imply BIS endorsement. Commercial products must not charge extra for the BIS data. https://data.bis.org/help/legal

**Quality:** Policy rates are step series that only change on decision days. Series definitions change over time (the US series switches from fed funds effective to target midpoint in 1985), noted in the series metadata.
