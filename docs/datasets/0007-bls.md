# BLS Public Data API

## In plain words

The US government office that counts jobs and measures how fast prices go up.

## Summary

US consumer prices (CPI), jobs and payrolls, wages and producer prices, at full detail.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** US government data, generally public domain. See the API terms of service at https://www.bls.gov/developers/termsOfService.htm and linking and copyright info at https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm

**Quality:** Serves the latest revised values only. Seasonal factors are revised each year. CPI for September 2026 pulled with no key (tested).
