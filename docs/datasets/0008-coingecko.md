# CoinGecko API

## In plain words

A website that tracks the price of almost every cryptocurrency and lets programs ask for it.

## Summary

Prices, market caps and volumes for thousands of coins, averaged across exchanges.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Attribution required on the free plan. Commercial use is allowed with attribution, but reselling or redistributing API access is not. https://www.coingecko.com/en/api/pricing

**Quality:** Aggregated prices, not one exchange's trades. One year of history on the free plan is short.
