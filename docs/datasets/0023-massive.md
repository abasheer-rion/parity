# Massive (formerly Polygon.io)

## In plain words

A market data company with a free plan for currency prices, but it only goes back two years.

## Summary

All forex and crypto tickers with end of day data, minute bars, reference data and technical indicators on the free plan. Polygon renamed itself Massive on 2025-10-30; old keys and api.polygon.io still work.
