# Kenneth R. French Data Library

## In plain words

Two famous professors post free files showing how different kinds of stocks did each month back to 1926.

## Summary

Fama and French equity factors (market, size, value, profitability, investment, momentum), the risk free rate and many sorted and industry portfolios, for the US and international markets.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** Marked Copyright Eugene F. Fama and Kenneth R. French, with no explicit license. Widely used in research with citation; do not redistribute the files. https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html

**Quality:** The full history is rebuilt every month, so past returns can change when CRSP revises its data or the method changes (major changes logged in 2012 to 2021). Equity only, no currencies.
