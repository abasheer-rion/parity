# CFTC Commitments of Traders

## In plain words

Every week the US futures regulator shows who is betting on each currency going up or down.

## Summary

Weekly open interest and long and short positions by trader type for US futures, including the CME currency futures (EUR, JPY, GBP and others). Socrata dataset r4w3-av2u, plus plain text files.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** US federal government data. No terms of use were quoted on the COT page (checked 2026-10-21). The API needs no token "as long as usage is reasonable". Not yet used, so not yet checked further.

**Quality:** Futures only, a small slice of the FX market. Positions as of Tuesday, released Friday.
