# TrueFX historical downloads

## In plain words

A company that gives away old second by second currency prices from big banks if you make a free account.

## Summary

Free top of book tick data with bid and offer in millisecond detail, sourced from Integral's network of institutions. Live data is a paid product from 4,950 USD a month.

## Terms and quality

**Terms:** The download page says "Please log in or register for free access to the historical data downloads." The terms sit behind the login, so we do not automate it. A person may download files and import them with parity ticks --import.

**Quality:** Monthly CSV files per pair, so large downloads. Pair list and start date not confirmed on TrueFX's own pages.
