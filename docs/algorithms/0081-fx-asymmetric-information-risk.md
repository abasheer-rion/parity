## How it works

**Simple:** In some currency pairs, trades push the price and it stays pushed, which means some traders know more than others. Buy those pairs and sell the pairs where trades barely move prices for good.

<details><summary>Analogy</summary>

Like a card game where some players can see more cards: you ask for a better deal to sit at that table.

</details>

<details><summary>Precise</summary>

For each pair, estimate the permanent price impact of order flow from CLS data, averaged across participant groups. Each month long the top third and short the bottom third, equal weights, dollar neutral, via forwards.

</details>

### The rule

Monthly: estimate permanent price impact per pair from the past month's order flow. Long the top tertile, short the bottom tertile. Hold one month.

### Data needed

- Order flow by pair and participant type (CLS, paid)
- Spot and forward quotes with bid and ask

### Why it might work

Holding currencies with more informed trading is riskier for uninformed investors, so they earn a premium.

### Why it might fail

Short sample (2012-2019) and paid data; we found no outside replication.
