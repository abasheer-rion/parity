## How it works

**Simple:** Thousands of people build prediction models on the same disguised stock data and bet tokens on them. The company blends the best-backed predictions into one model and trades with it.

<details><summary>Analogy</summary>

Like a quiz show where the audience votes with real money and the host follows the weighted vote.

</details>

<details><summary>Precise</summary>

Participants train models on obfuscated stock features to predict 20 or 60 day targets, stake NMR on their predictions, and are paid or burned according to correlation (CORR) and meta model contribution (MMC). Only staked predictions are stake-weighted into the meta model that drives a global equity fund.

</details>

### The rule

For a replication, download the free Numerai dataset, train a gradient boosting model on the features to predict the main target, rank predictions each round, and evaluate per-era correlation with the target; there is no public way to trade the meta model itself.

### Data needed

- Numerai tournament data (free, obfuscated features and targets)
- numerapi client

### Why it might work

Averaging many weakly correlated models and paying for unique contribution can reduce overfitting and find signals a single team misses.

### Why it might fail

Features are obfuscated, so participants cannot relate signals to economics, and per-era correlations are small and noisy. Fund returns are reported by the manager, not audited publicly.
