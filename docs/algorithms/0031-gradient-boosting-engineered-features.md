## How it works

**Simple:** You turn price history into many hints, like how far price is from its recent average, and let a tree-based model learn which hints predict the next 15 minutes.

<details><summary>Analogy</summary>

Like a weather forecaster who combines dozens of small clues (wind, clouds, pressure) instead of relying on one.

</details>

<details><summary>Precise</summary>

Predict 15-minute residualized returns for 14 coins from minute bars with LightGBM on lagged price, volume and moving-average features; validate with walk-forward grouped folds with gaps. Scored by weighted Pearson correlation over three months of live data.

</details>

### The rule

Every minute, compute lagged return and moving-average-ratio features per coin, predict the 15-minute residual return with a LightGBM model retrained on an expanding window, and hold positions proportional to the cross-sectionally demeaned prediction.

### Data needed

- Minute OHLCV for liquid coins (Kaggle dataset or exchange APIs)
- Coin weights for the residualized target

### Why it might work

Short-horizon return predictability in crypto from reversal, momentum and cross-asset lead-lag can be captured by nonlinear models; G-Research notes feature work mattered more than model choice.

### Why it might fail

Winning live correlations in such competitions are small and the leaderboard was noisy, so ranks partly reflect luck. 15-minute signals need very low costs, and the edge may vanish after fees.
