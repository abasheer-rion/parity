# The dollar weakens on scheduled Fed decision days

## Claim

Being short the dollar against high-rate currencies on the eight scheduled FOMC decision days each year earns a return that is large for the risk taken, and this was still true after the effect was published in 2017.

## Why it might be true

Mueller, Tahbaz-Salehi and Vedolin (2017) found it, linked to uncertainty about policy being resolved on those days.

## Why it might be false

It was published, and published effects tend to shrink. Eight days a year also means few observations.

## How we test it

Needs the historical FOMC calendar as a data series. Then a calendar strategy that only holds positions on those days. Report results before and after 2017 separately.

## Result so far

None yet.
