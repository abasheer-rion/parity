# Currency trends persist over months

## In plain words

The idea: if a currency has been getting stronger for a year, it will probably keep getting stronger for a while, like a ball that keeps rolling. This has worked in many markets in the past, but less so in currencies lately.

## Claim

A G10 currency that has risen against the dollar over the past 12 months tends to keep rising over the next month, enough to earn a positive return after costs when each currency is sized to equal risk.

Code: `engine/src/parity/strategies/fx_momentum.py` (strategy id `fx_tsmom`).

## Why it might be true

- Time-series momentum shows up across asset classes over a century of data (Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen, 2012; Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen, 2017).
- Central bank policy cycles last years, which can create slow-moving trends in currencies.
- It has historically done well when stocks crash, which would make it useful next to the S&P 500 even with a lower Sharpe.

## Why it might be false

- Profits from technical FX rules have shrunk over time (Neely, Weller and Ulrich, 2009).
- Hutchinson et al. (2022) found currency momentum turned negative after publication.

## How we test it

Same steps as [HYP-0001](0001-carry-still-pays-after-costs.md#how-we-test-it), plus a check of its correlation with SPY in the worst SPY months.

## Result so far

[EXP-0001](../experiments/0001-baseline-backtests.md): Sharpe 0.17 against 0.50 for SPY, and a 30% drawdown at only 6% volatility. Correlation with SPY is slightly negative (minus 0.12). The signal has a small, consistent rank IC (0.015 at one day rising to 0.037 at one month). Weak, as the literature predicted.

[EXP-0002](../experiments/0002-baseline-gauntlet.md), the gauntlet: fails all six tests against the S&P 500, passes three of five reality tests. Almost all of its return came from 2000 to 2008 (Sharpe 0.67, then minus 0.26 in 2009 to 2019). It is the only strategy that made money on average in the stock market's worst months, which keeps it interesting for [HYP-0007](0007-sp-500-plus-fx-beats-sp-500-alone.md).
