# An equal-weight energy book pays after 2014

## In plain words

Four ideas for choosing between oil, heating oil, gasoline and gas futures each have some published evidence. Each is weak on its own, and some of them often disagree. We give each contract a score on all four ideas, average the scores with equal weights fixed in advance, and hold the contracts in proportion. The test only uses the ten years after the newest paper's data ended.

## Claim

From 2014-03-03 to 2024-04-05, `energy_book` on rolled NYMEX crude oil, heating oil, RBOB gasoline and natural gas futures has a Sharpe ratio reliably above zero after 3 basis points of cost per unit traded and 2 basis points per roll, and passes at least four of the five reality tests in [REF-0006](../reference/0006-the-gauntlet.md).

The book, every month and for each contract that has all four inputs:

1. **Carry:** rank of 12 * ln(C2 / C3), as in `energy_carry` ([HYP-0013](0013-energy-futures-carry-pays.md)).
2. **Basis-momentum:** rank of the 12-month return of holding contract 2 minus that of holding contract 3 ([ALG-0088](../algorithms/0088-commodity-basis-momentum.md)).
3. **Value:** rank of minus the log of today's contract 2 price over its average 4.5 to 5.5 years ago ([ALG-0089](../algorithms/0089-commodity-value-reversal.md)).
4. **Trend:** the sign of the 12-month return of holding contract 2, as in `energy_tsmom` ([HYP-0012](0012-energy-futures-trend-pays-on-tradable-prices.md)).

Ranks are mapped to -1 for the lowest and +1 for the highest. The four scores are averaged with equal weights ([ALG-0098](../algorithms/0098-equal-weight-style-combination.md)), multiplied by inverse 60-day volatility, and scaled to a gross exposure of 1.

## Why it might be true

- Combining styles that disagree is where the published strength comes from. In commodities, value had a Sharpe ratio of 0.26 and momentum 0.53, with a correlation of -0.39, and the 50/50 mix had 0.71 over 1972 to 2011 [@asness2013] ([RES-0016](../research/0016-combining-many-strategies-the-evidence-and-the-ingredient-list.md)).
- Equal weights did as well as weights chosen from past data for five commodity signals over 1989 to 2017 [@fernandezperez2019].
- Basis-momentum was the strongest commodity signal we found: Sharpe 0.92 over 1960 to February 2014 for 21 commodities [@boons2019] ([RES-0015](../research/0015-lesser-known-strategies-and-what-firms-have-published.md)). Our whole window comes after that sample.
- Averaging scores before trading nets out signals that disagree, so the book trades less than four separate strategies would.

## Why it might be false

- **Four contracts.** The papers rank 20 or more commodities. With four, one rank step is a big change in position, and the four contracts all move with oil.
- **Contracts 2 and 3, not 1 and 2.** Boons and Prado use the nearest and second-nearest contracts. We use the second and third, the same pair our carry signal uses, so the book never touches a contract in its last month (the negative WTI price of 20 April 2020 is in contract 1). That is our own choice and may weaken the signal.
- **Value on futures prices.** The published value signal uses spot prices; we use contract 2.
- **Two of the four ingredients are already registered alone** (REG-0007), so their results may be known by the time this one runs.
- **Ten years is short** for a monthly strategy with four contracts.

## How we test it

1. Pre-registered in `REG-0009`. Every setting is fixed in the registration. There is no choice of weights and no dropping of ingredients after a result is seen ([RES-0016](../research/0016-combining-many-strategies-the-evidence-and-the-ingredient-list.md), rules 1 to 3).
2. Pass bar: at least 4 of 5 reality tests.
3. `prior_trials` counts every earlier registration, including `energy_tsmom` and `energy_carry` in REG-0007. Basis-momentum and value have never been run alone, so they add nothing beyond the book itself.
4. Results in [EXP-0010](../experiments/0010-equal-weight-energy-book-march-2014-to-april-2024.md).

## Result so far

None yet. Registered on 2026-10-23; no performance number has been computed for this rule or for basis-momentum or value on these contracts by anyone on the team.
