# Emerging market carry pays after costs

## In plain words

Countries like Brazil, Turkey and South Africa pay much more interest than the US. If we hold their money, spread over many countries, and only when the extra interest is worth the trading cost, we should earn something after costs. Every study that says so stops by 2010, so we test only the years since.

## Claim

From February 2010, a long-only basket of the third of emerging market currencies with the highest interest rate gap to the dollar (at least 1 percentage point), equal weighted and rebalanced monthly, has a Sharpe ratio reliably above zero after 10 basis points of cost per unit traded, and passes at least four of the five reality tests in [REF-0006](../reference/0006-the-gauntlet.md).

## Why it might be true

- Adding EM currencies raised carry's Sharpe ratio from 0.56 to 0.74 after costs over 1983 to 2009 (Menkhoff et al., 2012), and a cost-aware rule earned 1.32 over 1997 to 2006 (Burnside et al., 2007); see [RES-0012](../research/0012-emerging-market-currencies.md).
- EM currencies move less together than G10 currencies, so a basket diversifies.
- Our G10 carry was small but consistent ([EXP-0004](../experiments/0004-all-strategies-1999.md)).

## Why it might be false

- The 2010s were bad for EM currencies: the 2013 taper scare, the 2014 to 2016 commodity slump, Turkey and Argentina in 2018, Covid in 2020, Russia in 2022.
- In EM, high rates partly predict depreciation, as theory says (Frankel and Poonawala, 2010).
- Our free rates are policy or interbank rates, not what forwards pay, and the ruble's 2022 exit is booked at its last ECB price, which flatters the result.

## How we test it

1. Pre-registered in `REG-0006` before any performance number was computed: start 2010-02-01 (after every paper's sample), 10 bp cost per unit traded, defaults as in the code.
2. Pass bar: at least 4 of the 5 reality tests (B1 to B5). The six beat-the-market tests are reported too.
3. Trials: counted with every earlier trial in the project, as in `REG-0003`.
4. Results in [EXP-0007](../experiments/0007-emerging-market-currencies-since-2010.md).

## Result so far

None yet. Registered on 2026-10-21; no performance number has been computed for this rule by anyone on the team.
