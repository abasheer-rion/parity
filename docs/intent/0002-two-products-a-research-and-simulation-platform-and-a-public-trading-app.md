# Two products, a research and simulation platform and a public trading app

## In plain words

Parity started as a research project asking whether a currency strategy can beat the S&P 500 ([INT-0001](0001-initial-intent.md)). On 21 October 2026 the owner widened it into two products. The first is a research and simulation platform: the research we already do, plus a "world sim" where people and AI models can play out events, from a single commodity price change to a war, see the risks, and test trading ideas on simulated data. The second is a public app in the spirit of Public and QuantPad: open to anyone, easy to use, AI driven, where people build, test and understand strategies. INT-0001 still holds for the research; this adds where it is going.

## Summary

| Product | Who it is for | What it does | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research and simulation | The team first, then researchers and AI agents | Papers, glossary, strategy catalog and tests, the gauntlet, and a world simulator for micro and macro scenarios | A separate subdomain for the world sim (proposed `sim.endyland.com`), research at `parity.endyland.com` |
| Public app | Anyone with an account | Accessible, AI driven: build and test strategies by asking in plain words, on-demand indicators and KPIs, clear explanations, honest results | `parity.endyland.com` after sign-in |

## What we want

1. **Match what QuantPad offers, for currencies and macro, without its limits.** An AI helper that writes and tests strategies against our own engine, on-demand indicators and KPIs, and data people can export. A review on 2026-10-21 found QuantPad targets futures and options prop-firm traders, has no spot currency data and forbids data export; building our own was estimated at 8 to 12 days.
2. **Many more strategies.** Sixteen tested strategies is far too few. Use the catalog (now over 80 entries), the paper library ([RES-0010](../research/0010-paper-library-fx-and-market-microstructure.md)) and the `parity new-strategy` and `parity analyse` commands to add them steadily, each through the same tests.
3. **A dashboard people can find their way around.** Today it is messy: it is unclear where to go, what terms mean, what search finds, and what the reports and papers are saying. Every page should say what it is for, every number should explain itself, and search should answer questions, not just match words.
4. **A world simulator as its own product.** Prices of commodities, currencies and stocks responding to events at micro and macro scale; scenarios that AI models can run, compare and learn risk from; simulated data that AI trading models can be tested on before any real money. It grows from the scenario tool ([REF-0011](../reference/0011-scenario-simulator-method.md)), which today only replays what happened after similar past events.
5. **Public by design.** Open sign-up, accessibility, plain language, honest about uncertainty, AI driven throughout.

## What we do not want

- Claims that any tool predicts markets. Simulations show possible paths and their risks, never promises.
- Trading real money for users, or advice to individuals, before the legal and regulatory work is done. Regulation was out of scope for the research and must come back in for a public product.
- Data used against its licence. Every source keeps its terms ([REF-0010](../reference/0010-fx-data-sources-licences-and-resolution.md)).
- Dropping the research discipline: registered plans, the ledger and the gauntlet still decide what counts as a result.

## How we will know

- A newcomer can find a strategy, understand its results and the words on the page, and run a comparison without help.
- At least 100 strategies have been through the gauntlet, with names, tags and strengths and weaknesses.
- The AI helper turns a plain-words idea into a tested strategy report in minutes, with look-ahead checks.
- The world sim runs a user-defined scenario across commodities, currencies and stocks, shows a range of outcomes with their risks, and exports the simulated paths for an AI model to train or test on.
- Public sign-up works end to end, including email verification through Resend.
