# Equal-weight energy book, March 2014 to April 2024

## In plain words

We combine four published ways of choosing between energy futures into one strategy, with equal weights decided before seeing any result. The test only uses the ten years after the newest paper's data ended, which is also where the free futures prices stop.

## Question

On rolled NYMEX energy futures from 2014-03-03 to 2024-04-05, does the equal-weight average of carry, basis-momentum, 5-year value and 12-month trend scores pass at least four of the five reality tests, after 3 bp per unit traded and 2 bp per roll? Registered as [REG-0009](../../registrations/REG-0009-equal-weight-energy-book.json). Hypothesis: [HYP-0016](../hypotheses/0016-an-equal-weight-energy-book-pays-after-2014.md).

## Setup

- Prices and returns: the same EIA contracts and rolled indices as [EXP-0008](0008-energy-futures-trend-and-carry.md). `fut.<X>` holds contract 2. For basis-momentum the strategy builds the same kind of index holding contract 3, with no roll cost because it is only a signal.
- Window: the first business day after the basis-momentum paper's sample (February 2014) to the last EIA price. Signals use earlier data as history: value needs 5.5 years, so it is live for every contract by the start. Gasoline begins in October 2005, which gives it 8.4 years of history at the start.
- `energy_book`: for each contract with all four inputs, the average of carry, basis-momentum and value ranks (each mapped to -1..+1) and the 12-month trend sign. Then inverse 60-day volatility weights, gross exposure 1, monthly. The registered defaults are `lookback` 252 (trend and basis-momentum) and monthly rebalancing. The gauntlet's sensitivity test also tries `lookback` 126 and 504.
- Costs: 3 bp per unit traded, 2 bp per roll, as in REG-0007.
- No strategy return was computed before registering. The only checks were unit tests on made-up prices: each ingredient points the right way, the weights are equal, and the strategy does not look ahead.

## Threats to validity

- **Four correlated contracts.** Ranking four energy contracts is a weak version of the papers' 20 or more commodities.
- **Signal pair.** Basis-momentum uses contracts 2 and 3, not 1 and 2 as published, so that no input reads a contract in its last month.
- **Known ingredients.** `energy_tsmom` and `energy_carry` (REG-0007) may have been run by the time this runs, and their results will be known. The combination rule cannot change after that, but readers should know.
- **Natural gas seasonality** moves both its carry and its value signals.
- **Data ends in April 2024**, so there is no forward test with this source.
