# Rerun of all sixteen strategies with corrected scoring

## In plain words

Our independent audit found two mistakes in how we scored strategies, which made stock timing rules look better and currency strategies worse than they are. We fixed the code. This experiment reruns the same sixteen strategies, with the same settings, so the official record matches the fixed scoring.

## Question

With Sharpe ratios measured on returns above the T-bill and borrowing paid for ([DEC-0009](../decisions/0009-excess-returns-financed-leverage-frozen-code.md)), does any of the sixteen strategies of [EXP-0004](0004-all-strategies-1999.md) pass all six A tests? Registered as [REG-0004](../../registrations/REG-0004-rerun-with-corrected-scoring.json).

## Setup

- The sixteen strategies and settings of `REG-0003`, unchanged.
- 1999-01-04 to the latest data, 1 bp costs, carry credited.
- 290 trials counted (188 before, 86 strategy factory recipes from [DEC-0014](../decisions/0014-a-strategy-factory-and-an-always-on-search.md), plus these 16).

## Results

## Threats to validity

- Every strategy here was seen under the old scoring, so this is a correction of the record, not a fresh test.
- The audit's H1 and H2 findings (the gauntlet has little power, and one trial count across all families sets the luck bar) are still open. Results should be read with them in mind.
- `commodity_tsmom` still uses spot prices; its fair retest is [EXP-0008](0008-energy-futures-trend-and-carry.md).
