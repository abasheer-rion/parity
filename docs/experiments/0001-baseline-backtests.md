# Baseline backtests of carry, trend and the commodity gap

## In plain words

Our first test. We ran three ideas over 26 years of real data with standard settings and compared them with just holding the stock market. All three did worse. That is what most research predicted, and it gives us a starting line.

## Question

Before tuning anything, how do the three starting strategies compare with the S&P 500 using textbook settings?

## Setup

- **Code:** tag `v0.1.0`, the first commit of the repo.
- **Data:** fetched 2026-10-20 with `parity fetch` (FRED for currencies, rates and commodities; Yahoo for SPY with dividends). See [REF-0003](../reference/0003-data-sources.md).
- **Period:** 2000-01-03 to 2026-10-20. The commodity gap starts 2001-03 because it needs a year of history for its estimates.
- **Costs:** 1 bp per unit traded, carry credited daily from 3-month interbank rates.
- **Commands:**

```bash
parity run                                   # raw risk
parity run --target-vol 0.15 --out output/vol15   # scaled to 15% volatility
```

- **Trials counted for the deflated Sharpe ratio:** 3.

Every number here comes from `engine/output/latest.json` produced by those commands. Parameters are the defaults in each strategy file. None were tuned.

## Threats to validity

The full list, with the stricter tests, is in [EXP-0002](0002-baseline-gauntlet.md#threats-to-validity). The short version: nothing here is truly out of sample, two interest rate series are stale in 2026, interbank rates overstate the carry a trader would earn, and the commodity gap compares prices taken at different times of day.

## What we learned

1. **Nothing beats the S&P 500.** All three fail the first test in [DEC-0006](../decisions/0006-how-we-define-beating-the-sp-500.md). That matches the literature ([RES-0001](../research/0001-reading-list.md#summary)).
2. **Carry is the strongest of the three** and cheap to trade, with a break-even cost far above real spreads. It is still well short of SPY.
3. **Trend is weak but slightly negatively correlated with SPY,** which is the property that matters for [HYP-0007](../hypotheses/0007-sp-500-plus-fx-beats-sp-500-alone.md).
4. **The commodity gap is the interesting failure.** Its signal looks more informative at longer horizons, but the strategy trades it weekly, so most of what it pays in costs buys nothing. This is exactly the kind of lead the dashboard's information coefficient chart is for. The two-instrument IC is pooled by year and uses overlapping returns, so the 63-day number is weaker evidence than it looks.
5. **Data quirk:** WTI settled at minus 37 dollars on 2020-04-20. The commodity gap now treats non-positive prices as missing.
6. **Two rate series stop at January 2026** (euro area and UK), so carry for EUR and GBP uses a stale rate for most of 2026. Flagged on the Data page.
