# Pre-registered test of the monthly commodity gap

## In plain words

Our oil idea failed when it traded weekly, but a monthly version looked a bit better, and we only noticed that after looking. So we wrote down a strict rule first: the monthly version had to pass at least 4 of the 5 "is it real" tests. Then we ran it on a fresh computer. It passed 2. The idea is retired.

## Question

Does the commodity gap, rebalanced monthly with every other setting unchanged, show a real effect? Pre-registered as [REG-0002](../../registrations/REG-0002-monthly-commodity-gap.json) for [HYP-0008](../hypotheses/0008-monthly-commodity-gap.md).

## Setup

- Strategy `fx_commodity_gap` with `rebalance: M`; all other parameters as in [EXP-0002](0002-baseline-gauntlet.md#setup).
- Pass criterion fixed in advance: at least 4 of the 5 reality tests (B1 to B5) in [REF-0006](../reference/0006-the-gauntlet.md).
- Trials counted: 13 (12 prior from the EXP-0002 sweep, plus this one).
- Run officially on GitHub Actions from a clean checkout, recorded in the ledger, with outputs and data attached to the release named after the run.

## Threats to validity

- **Protocol slip.** This registration was dry-run once on 2026-10-20 before it was committed, which our later rule forbids. The file was committed unchanged afterwards, so the plan could not have been tuned to that output, but we record it ([DEC-0007](../decisions/0007-official-results-come-only-from-the-ledger.md#consequences)).
- **Already seen.** Its full-sample Sharpe of about 0.26 was visible in the EXP-0002 sweep before registration. The registered bar was the reality tests, which had not been seen.
- **Same data.** No new data has arrived since EXP-0002, so this is not out of sample in time.
- All threats listed in [EXP-0002](0002-baseline-gauntlet.md#threats-to-validity) apply.

## What we learned

1. Trading the signal more slowly roughly quadruples its Sharpe (0.06 to 0.26) but it is still not distinguishable from luck: its bootstrap range includes zero and it does not clearly beat random-date copies.
2. The pre-registration did its job. Without it, a Sharpe of 0.26 after "discovering" monthly rebalancing could easily have been written up as a promising result.
3. Together with EXP-0002 this retires the commodity gap in its current form.

## Next

- Leave this idea alone unless a new hypothesis gives a different reason to expect it to work, for example the daily lead-lag tested directly, or a split before and after 2020.
- Move effort to [HYP-0007](../hypotheses/0007-sp-500-plus-fx-beats-sp-500-alone.md) and to the algorithm catalog.
