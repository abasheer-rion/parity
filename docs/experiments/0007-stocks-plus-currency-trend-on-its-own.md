# Stocks plus currency trend on its own

## In plain words

Currency trend made money in most stock market crashes since 1999. If you hold stocks and add some currency trend, the trend may soften the crashes. This experiment tests that combination on its own, with the exact claim from our hypothesis written down as the pass bar before the run.

## Question

Does `stocks_plus_fx_trend` beat the US market on Sharpe ratio (A1), make more money at the market's volatility (A2) and hold up in the market's worst months (B5)? Registered as [REG-0005](../../registrations/REG-0005-stocks-plus-fx-trend-on-its-own.json) for [HYP-0007](../hypotheses/0007-sp-500-plus-fx-beats-sp-500-alone.md).

## Setup

- Half the risk in US stocks, half in currency trend scaled to the stocks' volatility, the idle half in T-bills (the M1 fix in DEC-0009).
- 1999-01-04 on, 1 bp costs.
- Pass bar fixed in advance: tests A1, A2 and B5 all pass.

## Threats to validity

- The crisis behaviour that motivated this test was seen before registering.
- The gains depend on a handful of crises (2000 to 2002, 2008, 2020, 2022). Four or five episodes are a small sample.
- The same strategy also runs in `REG-0004`; this run adds one trial.
