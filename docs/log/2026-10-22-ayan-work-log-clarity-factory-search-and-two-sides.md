## Found and fixed

These are honesty fixes. Each one made a strategy look better than it really was.

- **Negative oil price.** US oil (WTI) closed at -$37 on 20 April 2020, which produced a 110% loss in a single day. A percent change through zero now earns nothing.
- **Copper look-ahead.** Copper is a monthly average, but it was dated to the first of the month. "Stocks while copper rises" looked like the best rule found, and the gain was gone once copper was used a week after the month ends. Copper is also no longer something strategies can hold.
- **Oil pair timing.** Brent closes in London hours before WTI closes in New York. Trading the gap between them on the same day faked a 1.2 Sharpe ratio, and it falls to about 0.5 when the trade acts a day late.
- **Cash-only fakes.** Search variations that almost never invest scored fake, huge Sharpe ratios of about 12.8. They are now rejected.
- **Factor costs.** Factor portfolios now pay an estimated trading cost, and zero-cost books are no longer charged for borrowing.

## Incidents

- **A password reached a log.** The search service's first attempt to publish to the site printed part of the database writer password into its log. The line was removed from the log. The code now prints only the error type and publishes only with a full database URL. The password counts as exposed and needs rotating; the steps are in its vault entry. Pull requests 20 and 21.

## Decisions

- Merge our own pull requests once checks pass, unless the owner says not to or the change might break something.
- Every try in the search is counted, and the luck check is recomputed with the total.
- Product and research are two apps. Research decides and the product presents.

## Open questions

- **The exposed password (owner).** Rotate the `parity_writer` password. Then store the full database URL as the vault entry `parity-supabase-writer-url`. Once that is done, the search's results reach the live site.
- **Delaying interest rates (owner).** The monthly interest rates behind currency carry should be used a month late, like copper. This changes existing results.
- **Spot gas.** Spot natural gas makes "Commodity trend" show -88.6% for 2026.
- **Earlier items.** Supabase sign-in still needs the owner, and sending email through Resend and switching off the Cloudflare login wait on that.
- **Next session.** The AI strategy helper, which lets someone describe a strategy in plain words and have it tested.
