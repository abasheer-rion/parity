# Everything runs by itself

## In plain words

The owner asked that everything should always run by itself, and keep running more. On 24 October 2026 the sims got their own always-on service, the strategy search learned to open new ground when it runs dry, and the site got a line that says whether both are alive. The design is in [DEC-0037](../decisions/0037-everything-runs-by-itself-and-the-search-widens-itself.md).

## What happened

| # | Step | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Self-widening search | When more than half the draws in 20 batches are repeats, it widens one level by fixed rules: finer settings, shorter and longer lookbacks, books on part of a universe, bigger blends. Level 0 draws the same 500 variations from a fixed seed as before, checked against the code on `main` |
| 2 | The luck check counts every try | The deflated Sharpe now uses every variation ever backtested, failed ones included |
| 3 | The sims service | `parity sims run --forever`: 1,000 new Bot Battle paths with crashes, 2,000 Sim World paths and the four site exports per cycle, then a 20-minute rest. One cycle of 100 paths took 27 seconds here; the site exports take about 18 of them |
| 4 | Service files | `scripts/sims-service/` copies the search service: launchd, `nice -n 10`, one CPU thread, a pull of `main` every 12 hours, no listener |
| 5 | Database | A new migration adds a public health table written only through one function, and a private table for the sims' summary. Checked on a local Postgres: the writer can report and insert, nobody else can, an unknown service name is refused, and only 20 snapshots are kept |
| 6 | Site | Bot Battle shows each bot's odds of being wiped out in a crash, the share that ended ahead, and where its account ended. The search page and that card show the health line, live, stale after two hours |

## Decisions

- The search widens on evidence (repeat share over a window), not on a timer, and by rules, never by hand ([DEC-0037](../decisions/0037-everything-runs-by-itself-and-the-search-widens-itself.md)).
- The sims keep totals and histograms, not paths, because the box's disk is nearly full.
- No forward paper-tests run yet: there is no command for them outside the signed-in site.

## Open questions

- The box's disk was down to about 300 MB free during this session. Both services keep their files small, but something else is filling the disk (owner to look).
- A command for forward paper-tests of the search's leads, so the sims service can run them too.
