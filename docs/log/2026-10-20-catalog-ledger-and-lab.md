# Catalog, ledger and lab

## In plain words

We built four things today. A catalog of 36 known trading algorithms and 36 data sources, each with its own report. A record book of official test results that cannot be quietly edited. A strict routine every idea must follow. And new dashboard pages to look at data, try settings, and read all of it. We also ran our first two official tests. Neither strategy passed.

## What happened

- Built the algorithm catalog (`catalog/algorithms/`, one report per entry in `docs/algorithms/`) and the data source catalog (`catalog/datasets/`, `docs/datasets/`). Entries were researched against primary sources; numbers not read on the source page are marked.
- Built the ledger, pre-registration and the official-run workflow ([DEC-0007](../decisions/0007-official-results-come-only-from-the-ledger.md)), and the research routine with nine stages that the docs check enforces ([GDE-0004](../guides/0004-the-research-routine.md)).
- RUN-000001 repeated EXP-0002 officially with identical results. With the honest trial count (39), carry's deflated Sharpe fell from 0.88 to 0.44.
- RUN-000002 tested the monthly commodity gap against a bar set in advance. It failed ([EXP-0003](../experiments/0003-monthly-commodity-gap.md)).
- Added dashboard pages: Catalog, algorithm reports (printable), Lab, Explore, Ledger and Data sources.
- Wrote [REF-0007](../reference/0007-ways-to-build-new-algorithms.md) on honest and dishonest ways to come up with new algorithms.
- Connected Supabase with a write-only login for the nightly job and read-only access for the team.

## Decisions

- Official results only come from pre-registered runs on GitHub Actions, recorded in the hash-chained ledger ([DEC-0007](../decisions/0007-official-results-come-only-from-the-ledger.md)).
- The commodity gap is retired in both its weekly and monthly forms.

## Open questions

- Our one protocol slip: REG-0002 was dry-run before it was committed. The rule now blocks this.
- The Explore page shows the CAD and oil correlation falling to about zero after 2024. That fits Rees (2025). Should it become a registered test of a regime change?
- Yahoo's terms forbid automated collection. We need a cleaner source for the S&P 500 benchmark.
