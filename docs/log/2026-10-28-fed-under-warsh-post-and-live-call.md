# Fed under Warsh post and the live call for the 28 October decision

## In plain words

On 28 October 2026 we published a news post on the Fed under Kevin Warsh and registered the news-impact engine's calls for that day's decision before the statement. After the statement the post was updated with the result: rates held at 3.75% to 4.00%, 10-2, with Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack voting for a hike. The 2-year Treasury yield fell 0.03 points, as we expected, while the 30-year rose 0.07.

## What happened

- The post is `content/newsroom/posts/2026-10-28-the-fed-under-warsh-heading-into-the-october-decision.md`. The live call is `event-graph/calls/fed_2026-10-28.md`, with one file of calls per scenario.
- The stock calls have not been scored yet.
