# Ayan work log, merges, domain, email and the two product plan

## Decisions

- The site moves to `parity.endyland.com`; the world sim is proposed at `sim.endyland.com`.
- Email is sent from `parity.endyland.com` through Resend; no domain purchase for now.
- Build our own AI strategy helper instead of paying for QuantPad.
- Two products: research and simulation, and a public AI-driven app ([INT-0002](../intent/0002-two-products-a-research-and-simulation-platform-and-a-public-trading-app.md)).

## Open questions

- The owner signs in to Supabase so the access token can be made; then connect Resend as the sign-in email sender, turn email verification on, push the email templates, set the site URL to `parity.endyland.com`, and only then switch off the Cloudflare login.
- Confirm Resend shows the domain as verified.
- Google and GitHub sign-in need their sign-in apps made by the owner.
- Proposed next sessions, in order: dashboard clarity, a strategy factory toward 100 tested strategies, the AI strategy helper, the world sim product, public app polish.
- Legal and regulatory work before any real money or advice to users.
