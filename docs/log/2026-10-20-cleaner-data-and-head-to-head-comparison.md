# Cleaner data and head to head comparison

## In plain words

We stopped using Yahoo for stock prices and switched to free sources that allow it. Our history now starts in 1999. We built twelve more strategies, tested all sixteen side by side, and wrote down how they compare. We also checked what trading courses and forums tell people against hard evidence. Nothing beats the stock market yet.

## What happened

- New benchmark from Fama and French plus FRED ([DEC-0008](../decisions/0008-benchmark-data-without-yahoo.md)). It tracks SPY with a daily correlation of about 0.98. Every run now cross-checks sources against each other automatically.
- New data: BIS real exchange rates for currency value, US Treasury yields and T-bills.
- Twelve new strategies: currency dollar carry, cross-sectional momentum, value, a carry plus trend plus value mix, RSI and moving average rules, five stock timing rules, a commodity trend rule and a stocks plus currency trend overlay.
- Official run RUN-000003 of all sixteen ([EXP-0004](../experiments/0004-all-strategies-1999.md)): none passes all six tests against the market. The stock timing rules come closest. Currency trend made money in four of the five worst stock market episodes since 1999.
- Comparison of the whole catalog against our own measurements ([RES-0007](../research/0007-comparing-every-algorithm.md)) and of what courses and traders believe ([RES-0006](../research/0006-courses-and-trader-beliefs.md)).
- Dashboard: new Compare page (league table, crisis table, correlation map) and source checks on Data health.

## Decisions

- Yahoo is a cross-check only, never an input to results.
- `commodity_tsmom` on spot prices is not a fair test and is not to be cited.

## Open questions

- Registration REG-0003's title says fifteen strategies; it lists sixteen. The plan is frozen, so the slip stays recorded here and in EXP-0004.
- Should the three stock timing near misses start a forward test now?

## Later the same session

- 18 retail favourites from forums and courses joined the catalog (72 algorithms in total), from the golden cross to ICT and prop firm challenges. Seven have no credible evidence at all, and the entries say so. Two (the golden cross and RSI) were already tested by us in RUN-000003.
