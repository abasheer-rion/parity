# Parity Verify built and shipped

## In plain words

On 24 October 2026 the first version of Parity Verify was built from its spec, [REF-0016](../reference/0016-parity-verify-mvp-spec.md), in two pull requests. You can give it a trade file, describe a bot or pick one of our strategies, and it says whether the record holds up. A file never leaves the browser. A result can be shared as a public badge and withdrawn later, and a separate box adds it, without a name, to a research dataset.

## What happened

| # | Step | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The checker at `/app/#/verify` (pull request #66) | Five file formats, the four questions, five cards, claim arithmetic, described bots and our own strategies. Acceptance tests 1 to 8 and 10 pass: in the test suite, and test 8 also in a real browser |
| 2 | The luck check ported to TypeScript | Matches the engine's Python to 3 decimal places on 20 series and tries from 1 to 10,000 |
| 3 | Badges, the public page and withdrawal | New tables with row level security, checked against a local Postgres in 34 cases (who can read, publish, withdraw, the daily caps, account deletion). Acceptance test 9 passes |
| 4 | Layout jitter | Verify and the badge page measure 0.000. The win-rate lessons jumped by 0.23 on phones while their picture played; that is fixed |

## Decisions

- How the verdicts rank, what counts as a red flag and how badges are stored are in [DEC-0036](../decisions/0036-verify-runs-its-checks-in-the-browser-and-holds-daily-prices-only.md).
- We hold one price a day, not daily highs and lows, so "perfect timing" cannot be checked on real uploads yet.

## Open questions

- A source of daily highs and lows that allows this use, so card 4 can run in full (owner decision).
- A lawyer's review of the dataset opt-in wording and the privacy policy's Verify section before launch.
