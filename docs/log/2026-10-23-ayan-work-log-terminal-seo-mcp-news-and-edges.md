# Ayan work log, the terminal, SEO, AI tools, news and edges

## In plain words

From late 22 October into 23 October 2026, following [LOG-0008](2026-10-22-ayan-work-log-wordmark-sim-world-markets-legal-and-redesign.md). Sign-in went live on Supabase, and the old Cloudflare login was switched off, so anyone can now sign up. Sim World became Parity Terminal at its own address. The news section gained an archive and an impact search. 17 new strategies were tested honestly, and an Edge Lab teaches others how to look for an edge without fooling themselves.

## Decisions

- Sign-in is open to everyone through Supabase. The Cloudflare login is off.
- The Search Console property is the URL prefix for parity.endyland.com, verified by a meta tag.
- AI tools connect through MCP. Each user brings their own model. ChatGPT needs the hosted version, which comes later.

## Open questions

- **SEC contact.** Set the `PARITY_CONTACT` repository variable so the ETF and filings pages and the SEC news fill in.
- **Cash return.** Older strategies earn about 0.26% a year of free return, because their cash earns the interbank rate but they are scored against the T-bill. Fixing it lowers some existing results.
- **Rates lag and spot gas.** Earlier owner decisions are still open.
- **News archive storage.** The archive lives in the GitHub Actions cache. A durable backup is needed.
- **EIA key.** A free key from the US Energy Information Administration turns on the oil and gas stock series.
- **Hosted MCP.** For ChatGPT, and as a one-click editor extension.
- **Real sign-up test.** It has not been done end to end. The owner trusts it.
