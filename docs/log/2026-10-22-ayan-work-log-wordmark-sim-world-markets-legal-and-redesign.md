# Ayan work log, wordmark, Sim World, markets, legal and the redesign

## In plain words

The second half of 22 October 2026, following [LOG-0007](2026-10-22-ayan-work-log-clarity-factory-search-and-two-sides.md). Helpers worked in parallel, each in its own copy of the repo, and each merged its own pull request once its checks passed. Parity got the team's chosen wordmark, a much bigger Sim World with its own full-screen page, markets for crypto, perps and ETFs, 151 more free data series, a calmer dark design without a top bar, and working account pages with draft legal pages.

## Found and fixed

- **Spot gas spikes.** These made "Natural gas into winter" lead the strategy race at +2,632%. The strategy was removed (pull request 28).
- **The search running dry.** It had tried about 10,000 variations and was drawing repeats. The space was widened (pull request 32).

## Decisions

- Sim World lives on its own full-screen page, opened in a new tab, at `sim.endyland.com`.
- The site is dark only.
- Data that a provider's terms forbid us to show stays off the site (REF-0015).
- Real trading needs a broker partner, licences and identity checks first. It is a future decision for the owner.

## Open questions

- **Attach `sim.endyland.com` to the site.** The safety check blocked doing it through the API. The Chrome window Claude controls is signed in to the wrong Cloudflare account (a work account), and Supabase is signed out there.
- **Finish Supabase.** Make an access token, then follow `auth/CHECKLIST.md`: connect Resend, turn on email confirmation, set the site and return addresses, run the end-to-end test, and then switch off the Cloudflare login.
- **Legal pages.** They are drafts. Fill in the legal name, contact email, governing law and Supabase region, then have a lawyer review them.
- **SEC contact.** Set the `PARITY_CONTACT` repository variable so the ETF and filings pages fill in.
- **Optional keys.** Free keys from FRED, EIA, BLS and BEA, and paid data plans; see REF-0015.
- **Perps data terms.** Hyperliquid's terms do not say whether we may show its data. Perps can be turned off with one setting.
- **The older "Commodity trend" strategy.** It still holds spot gas.
