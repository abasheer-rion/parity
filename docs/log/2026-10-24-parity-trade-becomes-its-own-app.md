# Parity Trade becomes its own app

## In plain words

On 24 October 2026 Trade moved out of the product app into a full-screen app of its own, Parity Trade, at /trade/ and trade.endyland.com. It has its own sidebar, settings and keys, a 1, 2 or 4 chart grid, an order ticket on the side and the account along the bottom. The design is in [DEC-0040](../decisions/0040-parity-trade-a-full-screen-trading-app-of-its-own.md) and the platform survey behind it in [RES-0021](../research/0021-how-trading-platforms-lay-out-a-workspace.md).

## What happened

| 6 | Moved code | `src/pages/trade/` became `src/trade/`; the demo account logic became one shared provider used by the ticket, dock, Portfolio, Orders and Strategies |

| 8 | Product app | One Trade entry that opens Parity Trade in a new tab; old `#/trade` links redirect into it |
| 9 | Routing | Middleware serves `/trade/` at trade.endyland.com and gates it like `/sim/`; sign-in comes back to `/` on that host |
| 10 | Checks | Layout shift worst 0.001 on the Trade pages at 1440 and 390 wide; no accessibility findings on the pages audited; screenshots checked at 1440 by 900, 1920 by 1080 and 390 by 844 |

## Decisions

- Trade is its own app, not a tab, and not part of Parity Terminal ([DEC-0040](../decisions/0040-parity-trade-a-full-screen-trading-app-of-its-own.md)).
- Tab stays a focus key for keyboard users; [ and ] move between charts.
- One-key orders are off by default; Shift+B and Shift+S open the ticket on that side.
- The Supabase sign-in redirect list already allows every endyland.com address, so trade.endyland.com needs no change there. The explicit entry was added to `auth/push_templates.sh` and `auth/CHECKLIST.md` to match how sim is listed; it was not pushed to Supabase in this session.

## Open questions
