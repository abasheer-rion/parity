# Ayan work log, front page, Trade, links, copy and apps

## In plain words

24 and 25 October 2026, following [LOG-0015](2026-10-24-parity-trade-becomes-its-own-app.md). The front page was rebuilt around Verify, with no top bar and plain wording. Trade became its own full-screen app at trade.endyland.com. Every page got share cards and links that survive log in. About 290 lines of slogan-style text were rewritten, and the rules were written down. Work started on a desktop app for Mac and Windows, and on splitting out the Academy and a strategy Workbench.

## Running by itself

- The strategy search passed 146,000 tries. It widens itself when more than half its draws repeat; about 18% repeat now.
- The sims service finished 6 cycles and 6,000 simulated bot paths.

## Incidents

- **A stale preview.** The owner's screenshots came from an old preview server left running by an earlier helper. It now serves the latest main from its own folder.
- **A usage limit** stopped the app helper once before it had saved work. It restarted from the beginning.

## Decisions

- Each tool that needs the whole screen gets its own app and subdomain: Sim, Trade, Academy and Workbench. The main site and product app keep the explaining; the tools stay terse.
- Site copy follows REF-0017: plain statements and real numbers, no slogans.
- The startup programs are named as text, not as backers or investors.
- Phones wait. The first app is an unsigned desktop app for Mac and Windows.

## Open questions

- **Signed-in check of Trade (owner).** No helper can sign in, so the signed-in views of Trade need a look.
- **Earlier items.** Lawyer review of the Verify dataset opt-in and privacy text, a licensed source of daily highs and lows, and the items in LOG-0013 to LOG-0015.
