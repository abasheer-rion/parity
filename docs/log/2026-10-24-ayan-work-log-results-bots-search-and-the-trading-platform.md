# Ayan work log, results, bot battle, search widening and the trading platform

## In plain words

23 to 24 October 2026, following [LOG-0010](2026-10-23-ayan-work-log-private-markets-academy-fixes-and-ui.md). Four helpers from the last log all merged their work: the Academy, the scoring fixes, private markets and the UI library. The owner then asked for trading-bot simulations that show win rate is not profit, shareable clips made from the sims, a wider strategy search, and a real trading platform with demo accounts. Helpers were started on each.

## What happened

| 1 | You decide: fix the open questions; build a modern UI | Scoring fixes merged (pull request 51). "Rising rates" turned out to be look-ahead and fell from a Sharpe of 0.26 to -0.03. The UI library merged (pull requests 53 and 55), with the worst layout shift at 0.018 |

| 5 | A real trading platform: candlesticks, indicators, trackers, demo accounts; real trading later | A helper is building pro charts on Lightweight Charts, watchlists, a screener, alerts, and Supabase demo accounts with forward paper-testing. In progress. Real trading stays a future decision |

## Decisions

- Demo accounts use play money and delayed data, stored per user under strict row-level security. No real-money code paths.
- Content clips are labelled hypothetical and educational. The owner posts them; we have no social account access.
