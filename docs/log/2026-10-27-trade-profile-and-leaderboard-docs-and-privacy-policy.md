# Trade profile and leaderboard docs and privacy policy

## In plain words

On 27 October 2026 we wrote the paperwork for the new Trade profile tab: the privacy policy section, the decision record and the list of legal questions still open. The database and the pages were built at the same time on the same branch.

## What happened

- The privacy policy gained section 5, "Trade profile and the leaderboard": what the private part shows and who can see it, what other signed-in people see on the leaderboard and what they never see, that it is off until you join, that the country is chosen and never inferred, how leaving works, and that nothing is copied. Sections 5 to 15 became 6 to 16. The lawful basis table, retention, the Supabase row, your rights and the California section were updated, and the date is now 27 October 2026.
- The California section said "for the purposes in section 4", which had pointed at personalization since that section was added. It now points at "Why we use it".
- [DEC-0076](../decisions/0076-trade-profile-a-private-demo-calendar-and-an-opt-in-leaderboard-ranked-by-risk-adjusted-return.md) records why the board is opt-in, ranked by the t-statistic of daily results with minimum trades, grouped by a chosen country's region, and built as a live view of totals.
- [REF-0020](../reference/0020-compliance-checklist-before-each-new-step.md) gained a table for the leaderboard and five questions for counsel, and its privacy section numbers were moved to match.
- The cookie policy and the terms were read and left alone: the feature adds no cookie or browser storage that we know of, and the terms' rules are legal substance.

## Decisions

- Consent is the lawful basis for being on the leaderboard. The private part relies on contract, like other features a person uses.
- Open legal questions live only in REF-0020, not on the public pages.

## Open questions

- Forged ranks, the "no ranking by return" rule, handle rules, minors, requests about public handles, and UK and EU promotion rules. All are listed in [REF-0020](../reference/0020-compliance-checklist-before-each-new-step.md#trade-profile-and-the-leaderboard-dec-0076).
