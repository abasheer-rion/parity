# LMAX, an institutional FX exchange with a broker for professional traders

## In plain words

LMAX runs a marketplace where banks, funds and trading firms swap currencies, and every order waits its turn in one public queue. A sister company, LMAX Global, lets experienced traders with at least $10,000 use the same marketplace. LMAX is also famous among programmers for a very fast piece of free software it gave away. Parity should learn from how LMAX makes fairness its selling point: no hidden right to refuse your trade, and the same rules for everyone.

## Summary

| --- | --- |
| What it is | LMAX Group: LMAX Exchange (an FCA regulated MTF and MAS regulated market for spot FX, NDFs and swaps), LMAX Global (an FX and CFD broker for professional clients), LMAX Digital (institutional crypto), plus newer Omnia Exchange and Kiosk |
| Who uses it | Funds, banks, proprietary trading firms, brokers and asset managers on the exchange. Group trading volume $5.3 trillion in H1 2026, record average daily volume $41 billion ([H1 2026 results](https://www.lmax.com/press-centre/lmax-reports-h1-2026-financial-results/)). | Pricing | LMAX Global: minimum deposit $10,000; variable spreads; commission depends on trading activity and is quoted on request; market data fees in a PDF for clients (checked 24 October 2026). No public per-trade price, so we cannot compare it |
| Markets | Rolling spot FX, metals, indices, commodities, crypto CFDs, perpetual futures (Mauritius entity); exchange side: spot FX, NDFs, FX swaps; LMAX Digital: spot crypto |
| Platforms | Web trader (Trading Portal GUI), iOS, Android, FIX 4.4, Java and .NET APIs, MT4 and MT5 through bridges |
| Signature features | No "last look" rejections; strict price and time priority; order matching under 50 microseconds; up to 15 levels of depth on mobile and 20 on the API; VWAP prices; 10+ years of tick history for sale |

## Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths:
- Fair execution as the core promise: one central order book, firm prices, no last-look rejections ([LMAX Exchange](https://www.lmax.com/exchange)).
- Speed: internal matching latency under 50 microseconds, 800,000 orders a second at peak (LMAX Exchange page).
- Engineering credibility: its Disruptor library has about 18,500 stars on GitHub, and Martin Fowler's 2011 write-up of its architecture is still widely read ([Disruptor](https://github.com/LMAX-Exchange/disruptor), [Fowler](https://martinfowler.com/articles/lmax.html)).
- Deep market data: level 1 to level 3 order book data, ITCH feeds, trade volume and flow insight, over 10 years and 800 TB of history ([market data](https://www.lmax.com/global/market-data-access/)).
- Matching centres in London, New York, Tokyo and Singapore; a public, detailed status page by venue ([status.lmax.com](https://status.lmax.com)).

Weaknesses:
- Not for beginners or retail clients: LMAX Global's site is "only directed at Professional clients" and needs $10,000 to start ([FAQs](https://www.lmax.com/global/faqs/)).
- Prices are opaque to outsiders: commissions are quoted on request.
- The mobile app is basic and has no ratings in the US App Store ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id884042608)).
- Few independent reviews of LMAX Global's platform; forexbrokers.com has no review page (404).
- Leverage of 100:1 to 200:1 on majors and gold in some regions carries large risk (FAQs).

## UX notes

- **Layout:** The web trader is a professional order book screen; mobile has a watchlist, depth, charts, positions, working orders and history. We could not log in to the web trader, so its layout is **unverified**. See [RES-0021](../research/0021-how-trading-platforms-lay-out-a-workspace.md).
- **Onboarding:** Demo account from the app or lmax.com/demo; live accounts need professional status (experience, finance job, or a portfolio over 500,000 euros).
- **Navigation:** Mobile uses a simple menu for VWAP, news and the economic calendar ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id884042608)).
- **Density:** High where it matters: up to 15 levels of depth on mobile.
- **Charting:** Basic: live and historical charts, zoom and pan, intervals from 1 minute to 1 day. Serious users chart elsewhere and connect by API or MT4.
- **Order entry:** One-click trading, close, cancel and partial close; take-profit and stop-loss contingent orders; strict price and time priority.
- **Shortcuts:** None documented.
- **Mobile:** Touch ID login, real-time account status and notifications; the same app serves live and demo accounts.
- **Speed perception:** Average order matching of 3 ms on LMAX Global, published on its site.
- **Trust signals:** FCA, CySEC and MAS regulation; published execution figures; MTF pre-trade transparency data; a venue-by-venue status page; a history going back to a 2013 management buyout from Betfair ([buyout](https://www.lmax.com/press-centre/lmax-exchange-looks-ahead-after-management-buyout/)).

## What Parity should copy, avoid or beat

- **Copy:** State the execution rules in one sentence on the order ticket (price and time priority, no rejections); Parity's demo matcher should say how it fills orders (Trade). [DEC-0034](../decisions/0034-demo-accounts-paper-trading-on-delayed-data.md)
- **Copy:** A public status page split by service and region (Terminal, Trade). [DEC-0024](../decisions/0024-parity-terminal-a-market-terminal-of-its-own-at-the-sim-address.md)
- **Copy:** Publish execution and spread statistics as proof, not just claims (Verify). [DEC-0032](../decisions/0032-parity-verify-as-a-data-and-api-business-with-a-free-retail-tier.md)
- **Avoid:** Hiding prices behind "contact sales" for a retail product.
- **Avoid:** A thin mobile app as the only easy way in.
- **Beat:** LMAX proves fair fills but not fair claims. Verify can check whether a strategy's record is luck, using the same "show the rules" spirit.
- **Beat:** Depth and order book views are for professionals only; Parity Trade can teach what they mean in context (Trade, Academy). [DEC-0040](../decisions/0040-parity-trade-a-full-screen-trading-app-of-its-own.md)
