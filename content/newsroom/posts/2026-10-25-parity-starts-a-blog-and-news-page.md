Parity now has two new public pages.

- **The blog** at [/blog/](/blog/) carries research write-ups, analysis, explainers and opinion.
- **The news page** at [/news/](/news/) carries company updates and press releases. Market headlines and their impact stay in the app, under [News](/app/#/news).

## How posts are labelled

Every post carries one of three labels, shown under its title:

| Label | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Fact-based reporting | What happened or what we found, with every claim sourced |
| Analysis | Our reading of sourced facts and data, with the reasoning shown |
| Opinion | A view held by the writer, argued from sources. Not a research finding |

Research, analysis and news posts must list their sources, numbered at the end of the post. Corrections are logged on the post with their date.

## Who writes them

Posts are signed by a person on the team, or by a house byline such as Parity Research when the work is shared. House bylines are never presented as a person.
