## How to read this

Yields are shown in basis points, where 100 basis points is one percentage point. Prices and indexes are shown in percent. A week is the last value against the value seven days earlier. Markets that do not trade every day use their newest value, so a few rows can end a day or two before the others.

One week says little about the next. These numbers describe what happened. They are not a forecast and not a reason to trade.

The same series, with longer history, are in [Parity Terminal](/terminal/). How each source may be used is listed on the [data catalog](/app/#/registry).
