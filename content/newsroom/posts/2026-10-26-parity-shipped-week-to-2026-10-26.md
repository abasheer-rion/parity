## About Parity

Parity is a research project that tests trading ideas in public and publishes the failures as well as the results. Every change is made in the open, in pull requests anyone can read.

The full list, with fixes and smaller changes, is on the [changelog](/changelog/). Questions go to the address on the [contact page](/legal/).
