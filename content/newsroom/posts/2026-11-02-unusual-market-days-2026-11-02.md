## How this is measured

A move is the change from the last close before the news to the close one trading day later. The usual daily move is that market's spread of daily moves over its history, so a reading of 3 means a move about three times as large as a typical day. Our [event studies](/app/#/news) look at many past headlines on the same topic, which says more than any single day.

This is a report of what happened, not advice and not a forecast.
