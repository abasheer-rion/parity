Three new pages are linked at the bottom of every page and from the Help menu of the desktop app:

- **Changelog** lists new features and fixes each week, with a link to each change.
- **Announcements** is where we post news like this, and a note for each new desktop version.
- **Status** shows any current problem, such as live prices not updating, and a history of past ones.

When something is wrong, a short notice also shows at the top of the site. You can close it, and it stays closed for that notice.
