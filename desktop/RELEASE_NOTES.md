Parity for Mac and Windows. The whole Parity site in one window, with a sidebar.

New in 0.1.2: the app loads the live site, so it always matches parity.endyland.com and new pages show up with no reinstall. Offline, it shows the copy it was built with and says so at the top. Research and education only. Not investment advice. Trade uses a demo account with delayed data and no real money.

Which file to download:

- `Parity-<version>-arm64.dmg`: Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)
- `Parity-<version>-x64.dmg`: Intel Mac
- `Parity Setup <version>.exe`: Windows

The app is not registered with Apple or Microsoft yet, so each computer asks you to confirm the first time:

- Mac: drag Parity into Applications, then right-click it and choose Open, then Open again. If the Mac only offers to move it to the Bin, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
- Windows: if "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
