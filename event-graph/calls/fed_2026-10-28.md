## My read of the driver (judgment, not the engine)

- **Decision:** hold at 3.75-4.00% (~90%, in line with futures). Two or three dissents for a hike (Hammack and Logan
  said rates are not high enough; Kashkari dissented in July).
- **2-year:** lean slightly lower, **about -0.03** (range -0.08 to +0.05), 55% that it falls. Reasons:
  - A December hike is ~75-80% priced, and Warsh refuses forward guidance, so he cannot confirm it. On the one Warsh
    meeting without projections (July), the 2-year fell 0.04 despite three hawkish dissents. It rose at the two meetings
    with projections (June +0.15, September +0.07), and this meeting has none.
  - Oil fell 6% on Monday, payrolls are weak, and an election is six days away.
  - Against: core CPI at 0.3% m/m and a hawkish statement or dissents could lift it. A surprise hike (~9%) would
    put it up about 0.10-0.15.
- **Risk the engine cannot see:** a long-end shock. In July the 30-year jumped while the 2-year fell, and stocks
  followed the long end. Today Fed independence (Cook) and fiscal worries sit on the long end. If the 30-year rises
  more than 0.05 while the 2-year falls, expect the duration calls to be wrong.
